Engineering reality over marketing claims

Most infrastructure comparisons focus on surface-level features, price points, or SLA percentages.That approach consistently fails enterprises running mission-critical, regulated, or latency-sensitive workloads.

Crafthost approaches comparison differently.
We compare architecture, control, accountability, and failure behavior — because those are the factors that determine whether systems survive real-world pressure, regulatory scrutiny, and unpredictable demand.

This page defines how infrastructure should be compared when uptime, performance, and responsibility truly matter.

Why Most Infrastructure Comparisons Are Misleading.

In practice, large-scale outages, performance collapses, and regulatory failures occur not because technology is missing, but because infrastructure is architected to tolerate failure instead of eliminating it.
Crafthost comparisons focus on what actually breaks systems in production.

Traditional comparisons often assume that:

  • Redundancy equals resilience
  • SLAs equal availability
  • Scale equals reliability
  • Features equal capability

The Crafthost Comparison Framework.

Infrastructure Integrity

Failure paths are eliminated through isolation domains, not masked with redundancy. Resilience is architectural, not probabilistic.

Deterministic Performance

Capacity is reserved, not oversubscribed. Latency, throughput, and I/O behavior remain predictable under load.

Network Discipline

Traffic classes are isolated. Routing paths are deterministic. East-west and north-south traffic are governed, not blended.

Security & Compliance by Design.

Security and compliance are embedded at the infrastructure layer — not bolted on later. Controls are enforceable, auditable, and jurisdiction-aware.

Operational Accountability.

Responsibility is explicit. When performance degrades or availability is threatened, ownership is clear and corrective action is immediate.

Sovereignty & Control.

Data residency, jurisdictional boundaries, and workload isolation are enforced by design — not by policy documents.

What Crafthost Comparisons Deliberately Avoid.

To maintain technical honesty, Crafthost does not base comparisons on:

  • Marketing feature matrices
  • Oversimplified SLA percentages
  • Shared-tenancy performance assumptions
  • Best-effort availability promises
  • Price-only cost narratives

These metrics obscure risk instead of revealing it.

How to Use This Comparison Section.
