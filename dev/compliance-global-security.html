Our Security Philosophy

Crafthost operates on a clear principle: strong security is the foundation of digital stability.Organizations working in sensitive, regulated, or high-impact environments require infrastructures that remain continuously protected against evolving threats.

Security is not positioned as a feature—it is embedded into every system, every process, and every global location. This approach ensures that every workload hosted with Crafthost remains resilient, compliant, and safeguarded throughout its lifecycle.

We maintain this philosophy to support industries whose operations demand absolute trust and uninterrupted reliability

Multi-Layered Security Architecture

Crafthost applies a defense-in-depth model across all regions and services.Physical protection, network security, infrastructure isolation, and continuous monitoring work together to form a unified, fortified environment.

Each security layer reinforces the next, ensuring that even if one is challenged, the others remain intact.This multidimensional structure ensures high availability, reduces exposure, and supports mission-critical operations.

Every component is engineered to deliver predictable, long-term protection in complex and fast-evolving environments.

Data Center Security (Tier-IV & Tier-V Engineering)

Crafthost operates across a curated global network of Tier-IV and Tier-V engineered facilities.These locations implement biometric controls, mantrap entrances, multi-layer surveillance, and restricted access protocols.

Power systems leverage redundant grid feeds, failover circuits, multi-path cooling, and seismic reinforcement.This architecture supports uninterrupted operations—even in adverse conditions or unexpected regional events.Every facility follows unified engineering principles to ensure consistent performance and compliance across continents.

Our footprint supports sovereignty requirements for organizations with strict regulatory mandates.

Network & Edge Protection

Crafthost safeguards its global network through robust perimeter defenses.

Enterprise-grade DDoS mitigation filters hostile traffic at the edge before it reaches internal systems. Firewall layers, segmentation, traffic inspection, and encrypted routing strengthen network integrity.

Global routing optimization reduces latency while preserving confidentiality across all communication paths.These measures create a resilient, high-performance network suitable for demanding enterprise workloads.Network protection remains a central component in ensuring long-term service availability.

Compute & Environment Isolation

All compute environments enforce strict isolation standards.

Hypervisor hardening, operating system policies, container segmentation, and bare-metal isolation protect workloads across all service tiers.

This approach prevents lateral movement, unauthorized access, or workload interference.Isolation applies to Managed Cloud VPS, Dedicated Servers, Private Cloud, Hybrid Cloud, and GPU/AI systems.Resource governance ensures predictable performance while maintaining compliance with regulated ecosystems.

Customers benefit from environments designed to uphold strict security expectations.

Encryption & Data Protection

Data at rest, in transit, and in active use is protected through strong encryption protocols.Transport Layer Security (TLS), secure negotiation channels, and disk-level encryption apply across all global regions.

Encryption keys follow controlled access governance and validated operational requirements.These safeguards ensure confidentiality while aligning with industry and compliance standards. Encryption policies support the needs of financial, healthcare, government, and enterprise customers.This unified model secures the entire data lifecycle

Identity Governance & Access Control

Crafthost reinforces identity protection with strict access controls and account governance.Multi-factor authentication, controlled credential rotation, and role-based permissions form the core access strategy.

Privileged sessions are logged, monitored, and reviewed to ensure traceability and accountability.Customers retain full control over internal user roles, application permissions, and operational access decisions. This governance structure minimizes access-related risks across all environments.Access control contributes to strong compliance and operational integrity.

Continuous Monitoring & Threat Detection

Crafthost monitors its global infrastructure in real time to detect anomalies, risks, or suspicious activity. Threat intelligence tools analyze behavioral patterns and system events across all operational layers. Automated alerts initiate timely investigation and escalation when required.

Monitoring outputs undergo regular review to support governance and continuous strengthening. This vigilance allows for proactive mitigation and early identification of potential threats.Monitoring serves as a foundational pillar for long-term stability.

Scope of Security Incidents

Security incidents include any event that may disrupt confidentiality, availability, integrity, or operational continuity.

These events may stem from vulnerabilities, unauthorized access, malware, DDoS activity, configuration exposure, or unusual system behavior.

Incidents involving regulated data follow specialized protocols aligned with legal and compliance requirements.The scope covers infrastructure, networks, management systems, APIs, and all customer-impacting services. Events are evaluated on severity and escalated through structured response mechanisms.Every incident follows a complete investigative and mitigation workflow.

Incident Response Lifecycle

Crafthost manages incidents through a disciplined and predictable lifecycle.

/

Identification

Monitoring systems detect anomalies or indicators of compromise.

Alerts are categorized, assessed, and validated before escalation.All findings are recorded for traceability.

/

Containment

Affected systems are isolated to prevent exposure or escalation.Short-term actions stabilize operations; long-term controls prevent recurrence.

Containment may involve segmentation, access restrictions, or controlled shutdown.

/

Eradication

Root causes are removed, malicious artifacts eliminated, and affected components repaired.

All configurations undergo verification to ensure no hidden persistence remains.

/

Recovery

Services return to stable operation under heightened observation.

Recovery steps follow controlled procedures to maintain integrity and avoid re-exposure.

/

Post-Incident Review

A structured review identifies lessons learned, defensive adjustments, and future preventive measures.

Reports may be shared with affected customers as required.

Severity Classification & Escalation

Incidents are classified by severity to determine escalation routes and response speed. Severity categories consider risk to availability, data integrity, compliance, and service continuity.

High-impact events trigger immediate senior-level coordination. Medium-level events follow rapid tactical response procedures. Low-severity events are resolved with minimal operational impact. This classification ensures structured, predictable, and consistent response.

Communication & Customer Notification

Crafthost maintains transparent communication throughout the incident lifecycle. Customers are notified if an event affects their systems, data, or compliance responsibilities.

Notifications include details of the event, risks, impact assessment, and recommended actions. Follow-up communication supports recovery, mitigation, and future protective steps. All communication aligns with GDPR and applicable regulatory obligations. Transparency remains a guiding principle throughout incident handling

Data Protection & Legal Compliance

Security responses follow applicable data protection laws and industry standards. Events involving personal or sensitive data follow structured GDPR-aligned requirements.

Legal review determines whether notification to authorities or affected parties is required. Incident documentation supports audits, certifications, and governance review. Crafthost maintains global alignment with evolving regulatory obligations. This ensures legally compliant management of all security events.

Shared Responsibility Model

Crafthost protects the platform, physical infrastructure, and core systems.Customers manage internal access rules, credentials, application configurations, and data governance.

This shared model ensures clarity in operational responsibility and strengthens overall protection. Customers should report suspicious behavior immediately and maintain hardened configurations. Collaboration ensures stronger security outcomes for both parties. This model aligns with modern cloud governance principles.

Security for SMEs & Enterprises

Crafthost supports the operational needs of organizations of all sizes.

SMEs benefit from enterprise-grade protection without managing complex internal security operations. Enterprises gain scalable, compliant, and globally unified environments suitable for high-load and regulated workloads. All customers operate on the same global-grade security foundation. This ensures continuity and stability across expanding operations. Organizations can grow confidently while maintaining strong governance.

Coordination With Security & Intelligence Partners

Crafthost collaborates with global security vendors, intelligence feeds, and compliance organizations. These partnerships enhance detection capabilities, classification accuracy, and preventive strategies.

Integrated intelligence supports better anticipation of evolving threats. External expertise contributes to a stronger, globally aligned security posture. Partnerships also reinforce transparency and accountability in incident analysis. This ecosystem-based strategy strengthens readiness across regions.

Continuous Improvement & Future Strengthening

Security is a continuous discipline, not a fixed milestone. Crafthost regularly updates its technologies, threat models, monitoring tools, and defensive strategies based on global threat evolution.

Incident trends inform policy adjustments and infrastructure enhancements. Training programs sharpen response readiness and operational resilience. This commitment ensures that security remains strong as new threats emerge. Our goal is long-term stability for every workload entrusted to our platform.

Final Statement

Security is the central discipline that defines Crafthost’s global platform. Our unified framework protects critical operations, sensitive data, and the infrastructures that support organizations worldwide.

Customers choose Crafthost because they require environments built for trust, continuity, and resilience—without compromise. This policy represents our ongoing commitment to safeguarding digital ecosystems across every region and every industry.
