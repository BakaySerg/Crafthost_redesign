Transparent Disclosure of Third-Party Providers Supporting Crafthost’s Infrastructure.

Organisations that operate in today’s global environment expect complete transparency regarding how and where their data is processed. Crafthost maintains a strict, structured approach to data protection, and part of that commitment includes identifying the subprocessors we rely on to deliver secure, high-performance infrastructure services.

This page provides a clear, continuously maintained overview of all third-party vendors engaged by Crafthost in delivering compute, storage, networking, security, monitoring, support, and operational continuity across our global platform.

2. Why Crafthost Uses Subprocessors.

To ensure reliability, security, and global performance, Crafthost partners with a select group of providers that contribute specialised capabilities such as:

  • Facility-level infrastructure
  • Power redundancy and physical security
  • High-capacity network transport
  • DDoS protection and routing resilience
  • Backup and disaster recovery systems
  • Monitoring, SOC tools, and compliance logging
  • Payment and operational support platforms

Each subprocessor is chosen through a careful assessment of security posture, compliance certifications, operational maturity, and long-term stability.

3. How Subprocessors Are Evaluated

Every provider undergoes a structured review process:

A.

Security Assessment

Verification of encryption standards, access controls, intrusion detection, and incident-response protocols.

B.

Compliance Validation

Checks for ISO 27001, SOC 1 & 2, PCI DSS (where applicable), GDPR alignment, and adherence to international regulatory expectations.

C.

Technical Capability Review

Performance benchmarks, latency analysis, redundancy design, and resilience under high-load conditions.

D.

Contractual & DPA Framework

Binding Data Processing Agreements (DPA) and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where required.

E.

Ongoing Monitoring

Annual audits, risk reviews, and continuous performance tracking to ensure ongoing conformity.
