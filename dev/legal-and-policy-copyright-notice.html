Protection of Intellectual Property and Digital Assets

Crafthost’s digital presence, platform materials, and service-related content are protected under international copyright and intellectual property laws. This page outlines the ownership rights associated with our website, documentation, branding elements, and digital assets. All users of the Crafthost website and services are expected to respect these rights and comply with applicable laws governing digital property, creative works, and proprietary materials.

All content published on the Crafthost website and associated digital platforms is the exclusive property of Crafthost SRL. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Website text, written content, and documentation
  • Graphics, illustrations, and visual elements

No additional rights are provided beyond this limited viewing access.

Users may not copy, reproduce, modify, distribute, or republish any Crafthost content without written permission. Prohibited actions include:

Any unauthorised use constitutes a violation of intellectual property laws.

All Crafthost names, marks, service titles, badges, and design elements are protected as trademarks or service marks. These identity elements may not be:
