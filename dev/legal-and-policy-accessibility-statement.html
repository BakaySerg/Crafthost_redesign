Commitment to Inclusive, Accessible, and Responsible Digital Experiences.

Crafthost is committed to providing a digital environment that is accessible to all individuals, including users with disabilities, assistive technology requirements, and diverse browsing needs. Our goal is to ensure that every visitor—regardless of ability, device, or context—can access our services, understand our content, and navigate our platform with ease and confidence.

This Accessibility Statement outlines the principles, standards, and measures we follow to ensure that our website and digital products meet recognised accessibility guidelines and remain usable for everyone.

1. Our Accessibility Approach.

Accessibility is an essential part of Crafthost’s design and development philosophy. We follow a structured approach that integrates:

  • Compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1
  • Inclusive design principles and usability best practices
  • Ongoing improvements based on user feedback and testing
  • Compatibility with modern assistive technologies

These principles ensure a consistent and inclusive experience across all Crafthost pages and applications.

2. Standards and Guidelines Followed.

Crafthost is committed to meeting internationally recognised accessibility standards, including:

  • WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the widely accepted industry standard
  • EN 301 549 requirements for European Union accessibility compliance
  • Best-practice UX and readability guidelines

The website is regularly reviewed to maintain alignment with these frameworks.

3. Technical Measures Implemented.

To ensure high accessibility, Crafthost implements several technical and design measures:

  • Semantic HTML structure and ARIA labels
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation
  • Text alternatives for images and icons
  • Readable typography and scalable font sizes
  • High-contrast visual design where appropriate
  • Accessible form fields and error indicators
  • Compatibility with screen readers and assistive tools

These measures support a broad range of user needs and device capabilities.

4. Ongoing Accessibility Enhancements.

Accessibility is a continuous effort. Crafthost maintains:

  • Scheduled accessibility reviews
  • Testing cycles across multiple devices and assistive tools
  • Prompt correction of identified accessibility issues
  • Design standards that prioritise simplicity and clarity

These practices help ensure long-term accessibility across the full platform.

5. Third-Party Content and Integrations.

Some areas of the website may include third-party tools or embedded systems. While Crafthost selects vendors with strong accessibility standards, full accessibility compliance may vary.

If any such component creates a barrier, users are encouraged to contact us so that alternatives or support can be provided.

6. Response Timeline and Support.

Crafthost aims to:

  • Acknowledge accessibility requests within a reasonable timeframe
  • Provide support or alternatives if a barrier prevents access
  • Include accessibility improvements in future development cycles

Our goal is to ensure that users can interact with our services without limitation.

7. Legal Framework and Compliance Scope.

This Accessibility Statement applies to:

  • The Crafthost website (crafthost.com)
  • Subpages under the primary domain
  • Compliance, documentation, and support portals

It does not cover external websites, partner platforms, or third-party systems not managed directly by Crafthost.

8. Updates to This Accessibility Statement.

Crafthost may update this Statement periodically to reflect improvements, legal changes, or newly adopted accessibility practices. All changes will be published on this page and take immediate effect upon posting.

Accessibility conformance is reviewed periodically but is not formally certified unless explicitly stated. All changes will be published on this page and take immediate effect upon posting.

Feedback and Contact Information.

Crafthost encourages users to report accessibility issues, barriers, or suggestions for improvement. Every request is reviewed by our UX and compliance teams to ensure a prompt and constructive response.
