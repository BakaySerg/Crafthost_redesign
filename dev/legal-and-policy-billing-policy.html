Purpose of This Policy

The Crafthost Refund & Billing Policy defines how payments, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are managed across all services. This policy was designed to create a predictable and transparent financial experience for customers operating in mission-critical environments.

Whether you are an SME, enterprise, institution, or organisation in a regulated sector, the billing structure ensures clarity at every stage of your service lifecycle. Our goal is to provide a reliable financial framework that matches the stability and professionalism of our infrastructure.

By using Crafthost services, you agree to the terms outlined in this document.

General Billing Principles

Crafthost bills services on a clear and consistent schedule, with all charges displayed transparently in the customer billing portal. Invoices include detailed descriptions of purchased services, renewal dates, and any applicable taxes or adjustments.

Billing cycles vary depending on the service type and subscription plan selected during purchase. Customers are responsible for ensuring that payment methods remain valid and up to date to avoid service interruptions. These principles maintain a smooth financial experience for all service levels.

Billing Cycles and Payment Terms

Crafthost offers monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual billing options depending on the service category.Each billing cycle begins on the date the service is activated and renews automatically unless the customer cancels in advance.

Charges are processed at the beginning of the cycle, and invoices are issued electronically through the billing dashboard. Payment methods include major credit cards, debit cards, and other regionally supported methods displayed during checkout. All payments must be completed by the due date stated on the invoice to ensure service continuity.

Automatic Renewals

Most Crafthost services renew automatically to prevent downtime or disruption to critical workloads.Customers may disable auto-renewal at any time in the billing portal before the upcoming renewal date.If auto-renewal is active, the system will automatically charge the default payment method on file.Services entering a new billing cycle become non-cancelable for that period once payment is successfully processed.

Automatic renewals ensure that essential systems remain active without interruption.

Late Payments and Account Suspension

If payment is not received by the due date, invoices are marked as overdue and the account may enter a grace period.

During this period, customers receive reminders allowing time to resolve the payment issue.If invoices remain unpaid after the grace period, Crafthost may suspend services to protect infrastructure integrity and resource allocation.

Suspension does not erase outstanding charges, and full payment is required before service restoration.Prolonged non-payment may result in termination of services and permanent removal of data in accordance with our data retention rules.

Refund Eligibility

Crafthost provides refunds under specific conditions designed to maintain fairness and transparency.
Refund approval depends on service type, usage level, and the timeframe of the request.Managed environments, dedicated infrastructures, and resource-intensive services follow stricter refund conditions due to provisioning costs.

Requests must be initiated through the billing dashboard or official support channels.
Our team evaluates each case to ensure fair and consistent decision-making.
Service-level failures are handled through SLA service credits where applicable, not cash refunds.

Non-Refundable Services

Certain services are non-refundable due to the immediate allocation of compute, network, or licensing resources.
These include, but are not limited to:

Managed Dedicated Servers

Due to provisioning, hardware allocation, and configuration time.

GPU AI Servers

Because of limited global availability, resource locking, and high-demand operational costs.

Private Cloud & Hybrid Cloud Environments

Provisioned exclusively for each customer and not shared or repurposed.

Migration Services

Once initiated, time and engineering effort cannot be reversed.

Add-Ons, Upgrades, and Fully Delivered Services

Including advanced security modules, backups, and paid support tiers.

Non-refundable services help maintain platform stability and support resource-intensive environments.

Refundable Services

Some services may qualify for refunds under limited conditions.
Refund eligibility depends on the timeframe between activation and the request.

Managed Cloud VPS

Refund requests are considered if submitted shortly after activation and before significant usage.

Standard Hosting Services (if applicable)

Refunds may apply if the service was not used or activated.

Mistaken Duplicate Payments

Fully refunded after verification.

Unauthorised or Fraudulent Transactions

Investigated and resolved on a case-by-case basis.

Each request is reviewed with attention to fairness, customer trust, and operational integrity.

Service Cancellation

Customers can cancel services through the billing dashboard at any time.

Cancellation stops future renewals, but does not automatically grant a refund for previously paid periods. Billing for the current cycle continues until the end of the paid term, after which the service is deactivated.Customers should back up all data before cancellation, as Crafthost may remove environments after the service ends.

Cancellations ensure proper resource reallocation and operational stability.

Usage-Based Charges

Certain services may include usage-based fees such as bandwidth, storage, or resource overages.
Usage calculations follow standard metering practices and are displayed clearly within the customer dashboard.Overage charges are billed at the end of the cycle and must be paid within the designated period.

Customers should monitor usage regularly to avoid unexpected charges.This structure supports transparency while maintaining platform reliability.

Pricing Adjustments

Crafthost may update pricing to reflect industry changes, infrastructure costs, or market conditions.
Customers are notified of material pricing changes before future renewals take effect.Updated pricing does not impact the current billing cycle already paid by the customer.

This ensures stability and fairness across all service tiers.Pricing adjustments maintain the long-term sustainability of the platform.

Taxes and Regulatory Charges

Invoices may include VAT, GST, or other regionally mandated taxes based on customer location and applicable laws.

Tax calculations follow compliance rules for each relevant jurisdiction.
Customers are responsible for ensuring accurate billing information to avoid incorrect tax assessments.

Taxable services are clearly marked within billing documents.
This ensures compliance with international, regional, and local tax regulations.

Disputes and Resolution.

Billing disputes must be submitted within a reasonable timeframe through the designated support channels.
Crafthost investigates each case thoroughly and provides a clear resolution.

Supporting documentation may be requested to verify claims or correct any discrepancies.
Upon completion, findings are communicated transparently and adjustments are applied when appropriate.
This structured process reinforces trust and consistency.

Fair, Transparent, and Reliable.

Crafthost’s billing and refund framework reflects our commitment to professionalism, clarity, and customer trust.
Organisations rely on our infrastructure for critical workloads, and they deserve policies that match the stability of our platform.

Through transparent billing cycles, fair refund practices, and structured procedures, we ensure confidence at every financial touchpoint. This policy stands as part of our long-term commitment to reliability, governance, and service excellence.
