Two Clear Ways to Work with Crafthost.

Crafthost separates infrastructure services into two commercial paths.

1. Orderable Infrastructure.

Standard and managed infrastructure services can be ordered through the Crafthost customer portal. These services are structured, configurable, and suitable for automated Checkout with available add-ons.

Orderable services include:

  • Standard Infrastructure
  • Standard Cloud
  • Standard Dedicated Servers
  • Managed Infrastructure
  • Managed Cloud
  • Managed Dedicated Servers

These services are suitable when the requirement is clear, standardized, and does not require custom architecture review before purchase.

View Infrastructure Plans

2. Custom Infrastructure Solutions.

Advanced services require individual request and review before pricing, deployment, or responsibility commitment.

Custom services include:

  • Private Cloud
  • Hybrid Cloud
  • Sovereign Cloud
  • Regulated Cloud
  • Enterprise Hosting
  • GPU & AI Infrastructure
  • Kubernetes & Container Infrastructure
  • AI & Machine Learning Infrastructure
  • IoT Infrastructure
  • Migration Services
  • Security & Compliance Requests

These services are not suitable for instant checkout because the correct solution depends on workload, risk, region, compliance, capacity, support model, and commercial scope.
