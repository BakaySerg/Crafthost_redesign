Direct Channels for Security, Compliance, and Legal Coordination.

Strong infrastructure relies not only on engineering, but on clear communication. Organisations working with regulated data, sensitive workloads, national-level systems, or enterprise governance requirements need a trusted, direct line to security and compliance specialists. This page provides a dedicated contact route for reporting security issues, requesting compliance documentation, or submitting legal notices related to Crafthost services.

Crafthost maintains a structured process for handling every request with precision, confidentiality, and time-sensitive responsiveness. Whether your organisation operates in finance, government, healthcare, energy, manufacturing, telecom, or emerging AI sectors, this dedicated channel ensures your inquiries reach the appropriate teams without delay.

1. When to Use This Contact Page

This channel is designed for high-priority matters that require careful, compliant, and traceable handling. It should be used when your inquiry relates to:

Security incidents or suspicious activity

Situations involving potential vulnerabilities, misuse, compromise, or abnormal system behaviour.

Compliance documentation or verification

Requests for GDPR alignment, ISO certifications, data flow descriptions, or audit support.

Legal notices or formal communications

Official correspondence requiring confirmation of receipt, such as regulatory notices or contractual matters.

Infrastructure trust, privacy, and data-handling questions

Topics related to data protection obligations, subprocessor transparency, and retention requirements.

Routine support matters should continue to be handled through the standard support portal, ensuring fast operational resolution.

2. Security Contact Guidelines.

To ensure a structured and efficient response, please include the following details when reporting a security concern:

/

A clear description of the issue

Specify what was observed, when it occurred, and which service or environment may be affected.

/

Relevant technical data (if available)

Headers, logs, timestamps, or screenshots that help accelerate validation.

/

Impact assessment

Any known or suspected operational impact affecting your systems or users.

All reports are handled under strict confidentiality, with priority routing to designated security personnel.

3. Compliance & Regulatory Requests.

Compliance-focused organizations can use this channel to obtain documents required for internal reviews, audits, vendor assessments, or regulatory submissions.

These may include:

/

GDPR documentation

Data Processing Agreement (DPA), Subprocessors List, and privacy governance structure.

/

Certifications and assurance reports

ISO, SOC, Tier classifications, security frameworks, and data sovereignty statements.

/

Operational transparency materials

Network architecture summaries, security controls, data retention practices, and operational reliability information.

All compliance requests are logged, verified, and answered in accordance with internal governance timelines.

4. Legal Notice Submission.

Formal legal communications, including regulatory correspondence or notices that require verifiable delivery, should be sent through this channel. Each submission is time-stamped, acknowledged, and routed to the correct internal legal or compliance contact.

For non-urgent contractual matters, customers should continue to use the primary account contact or dedicated account manager.

6. Response Expectations.

Crafthost applies structured response categories to ensure timely handling:

  • Security incidents: prioritised review with rapid response
  • Compliance documentation: processed within a defined review cycle
  • Legal submissions: acknowledged and forwarded to internal counsel
  • High-impact matters: escalated directly to responsible leadership units

These timelines reflect operational best practices for enterprise, SME, and regulated-industry environments.

7. Confidentiality & Multi-Layer Handling.

Every request through this page is treated with confidentiality and routed only to authorised personnel. Crafthost maintains layered verification steps to ensure sensitive details are handled securely and in alignment with internal privacy controls and regulatory obligations.
