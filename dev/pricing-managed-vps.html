Managed Cloud VPS.

When Virtual Infrastructure Must Perform — Continuously.

Enterprise virtual infrastructure failures are rarely caused by missing technology.
They occur when operational responsibility is fragmented, escalation paths are unclear, and infrastructure management is treated as a background task instead of an owned function.

For organizations where availability, performance stability, security posture, and response time directly affect customer experience and internal operations, unmanaged or lightly managed VPS environments become a business risk, not a cost optimization.

Crafthost Managed Cloud VPS exists to address this gap — providing engineer-led operations for production virtual infrastructure without forcing enterprises into hyperscaler complexity or full enterprise outsourcing.

What “Managed Cloud VPS” Means at Crafthost.

Managed Cloud VPS is not shared hosting with a support add-on. It is a continuously operated virtual infrastructure environment, owned and maintained by senior engineers.

Operational responsibility is shifted from internal teams and reactive tickets to a defined operations model with accountability for uptime, performance, and response.

You retain control over architecture and workloads. Crafthost assumes responsibility for keeping the environment operational, secure, and stable.

Managed Services Scope.

Operational Ownership

24/7 monitoring, proactive remediation, and incident ownership — not best-effort support.

Performance & Stability Management

Resource utilization tracking, capacity alignment, and performance consistency for production workloads.

Security & Patch Governance

OS-level hardening, patch coordination, and vulnerability response aligned with operational risk.

Change & Incident Coordination

Controlled changes, escalation ownership, and post-incident corrective actions.

What This Service Is — & Is Not.

This Service Is:
  • Designed for production workloads
  • Operated by real engineers, not first-line support
  • SLA-aligned with service assurance mechanisms
  • Suitable for SaaS platforms, internal business systems, and revenue-impacting services
This Service Is Not:
  • Shared hosting or unmanaged VPS
  • Hobby, test, or disposable environments
  • “Login and forget” infrastructure
  • A replacement for Infrastructure VPS automatic ordering

If operational responsibility is optional, this service is unnecessary.

Pricing Philosophy.

Managed Cloud VPS pricing is intentionally structured to scale with operational complexity and responsibility scope, not with raw resource counts.

Pricing reflects:

  • Environment complexity
  • Availability and response expectations
  • Security posture and risk exposure
  • Operational hours and escalation depth
  • Number of managed systems
  • Integration with external services or vendors

There are no artificial tiers or per-feature charges.
