Enterprise Security Compliance.

Operationalized Compliance Across Global Regulatory Frameworks.

Enterprise Security Compliance at Crafthost defines how security controls are executed, evidenced, and maintained to meet regulatory and audit requirements across global enterprise environments.

This page explains how compliance is operationalized, not how frameworks are interpreted legally.Crafthost assumes responsibility for executing infrastructure-level controls in alignment with regulatory expectations, while organizations retain governance and audit authority.

Request a Compliance Architecture Review

Executive Summary.

Crafthost’s compliance model is built for organizations that must demonstrate continuous, auditable security execution, not periodic checkbox compliance.

Baseline

GDPR-aligned operational controls.

Mapped frameworks

ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2, PCI-DSS, HIPAA (where applicable).

Approach

Control-domain execution with framework cross-mapping.

Audience

Auditors, CISOs, risk officers, regulated enterprises.

Responsibility Boundaries.

Compliance responsibility is shared by role, not duplicated by task.

Client governance includes:
  • Determining applicable regulatory frameworks
  • Defining audit scope and objectives
  • Engaging auditors and regulators
Crafthost execution includes:
  • Operating mapped security controls
  • Maintaining compliance evidence
  • Supporting audit inquiries at the infrastructure layer
