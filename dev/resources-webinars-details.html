Hybrid infrastructure can provide greater architectural flexibility, but it can also increase complexity when connectivity, security boundaries, monitoring and operational ownership are not clearly designed.

This session examines the technical and operational decisions involved in building a hybrid infrastructure model that remains secure, resilient and manageable in production.

Practical Decision Framework

Questions to Consider After the Session:
  • Does our current architecture fit the workload?
  • Are availability requirements clearly defined?
  • Are security boundaries appropriate?
  • Is infrastructure capacity aligned with future demand?
  • Are backup and recovery processes tested?
  • Who owns monitoring and maintenance?
  • Who handles incident escalation?
  • Are responsibilities fragmented across multiple teams or vendors?
  • Would a different infrastructure model reduce operational complexity?
  • Does the organization have the internal engineering capacity to operate the environment consistently?
