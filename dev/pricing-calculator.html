Initial Review
  • Your request is reviewed by infrastructure engineer, not an automated system.

Technical Scoping (If Qualified)

Who Should Use This Request.

This request is appropriate if:

  • Your organization operates production or business-critical infrastructure
  • Annual infrastructure investment typically exceeds €100,000
  • You require clear ownership, escalation paths, and accountability

Who Should Not Use This Request.

This request is not suitable for:

  • Hobby, test, or experimental workloads
  • Price-only comparisons without operational scope
  • Short-term or unmanaged resource needs

Requests that do not require managed operations are redirected early to avoid wasted time.

Privacy & Data Handling.

Quote requests are reviewed only by Crafthost engineers.
