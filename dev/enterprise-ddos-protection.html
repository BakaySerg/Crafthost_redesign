Enterprise DDoS Protection.

Owned Mitigation, Not Escalated Response.

Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks are not hypothetical events. They are operational realities that expose the weakest point in infrastructure ownership: During active attacks, incident response execution at the infrastructure layer remains owned by Crafthost.

Crafthost’s Enterprise DDoS Protection is designed for organizations where downtime is unacceptable, escalation delays are costly, and responsibility cannot be fragmented across vendors, providers, and support queues.

We do not sell DDoS tools. We own mitigation execution as part of infrastructure operations.

What Enterprise DDoS Protection Means at Crafthost.

At Crafthost, DDoS protection is not an add-on service or reactive escalation path. It is an embedded operational responsibility integrated into network design, traffic governance, and incident ownership.

This model eliminates common failure patterns found in traditional setups:

  • Waiting for third-party mitigation activation
  • Ticket-based escalation during live attacks
  • Responsibility disputes between providers
  • Partial mitigation that protects networks but not applications

Crafthost assumes full operational authority over detection, mitigation, and stabilization.

Operational Capability Signals.

Crafthost operates DDoS mitigation within infrastructure environments designed for sustained, high-volume attack conditions, including:

  • Multi-terabit traffic absorption capacity across managed network domains
  • Distributed enforcement at routing and ingress layers, not deferred escalation
  • Continuous mitigation readiness with detection-to-action measured in seconds
  • Attack mitigation executed by on-call engineers, not automated black boxes

These capabilities exist to support owned execution, not as standalone features.

Built for Attack Lifecycles, Not Event Alerts.

Crafthost’s model treats DDoS as part of a broader threat sequence, coordinating mitigation with traffic control, system stability, and security operations.

DDoS events rarely occur in isolation.
They often coincide with:

  • Application abuse attempts
  • Credential stuffing
  • Resource exhaustion
  • Distraction campaigns masking intrusion attempts

Scope of Responsibility.

There is no shared mitigation ambiguity during live incidents.

Crafthost is responsible for:
  • Traffic anomaly detection and classification
  • Volumetric and protocol-level attack mitigation
  • Network-level filtering and rate control
  • Application ingress protection coordination
  • Incident ownership and recovery validation
Client teams remain responsible for:
  • Business logic
  • Application design decisions
  • Security policy direction

Proven at Enterprise Scale.

This operating model is actively used by organizations running high-exposure, public-facing infrastructure where downtime has regulatory, financial, or national-level impact.
Typical environments include:

  • Financial platforms and payment systems.
  • Telecom and network service operators.
  • SaaS infrastructure providers.
  • Regulated digital services under continuous attack exposure.

The model exists to function under pressure, not just under normal conditions.

When Enterprise DDoS Protection Is Required.

This solution is designed for organizations that:

  • Operate always-on, internet-facing infrastructure.
  • Cannot tolerate mitigation delays or partial protection.
  • Require deterministic response ownership.
  • Have regulatory or contractual uptime obligations.
