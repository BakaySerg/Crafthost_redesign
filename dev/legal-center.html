Our Approach to Legal Transparency

Crafthost operates under internationally recognized standards and adheres to a strict legal framework designed to protect data, support regulatory obligations, and ensure consistent, reliable service delivery.

Core principles include:

Clarity

– legal documents are written in accessible, structured language.

Integrity

– all commitments are honored under the highest industry standards.

Privacy-First

– customer data remains protected by strict access, security, and governance controls.

Regulatory Alignment

– operations follow GDPR, ISO frameworks, and global best practices.

Accountability

– defined responsibilities guide how data is processed and how service obligations are delivered.

These principles are reflected across all compliance documents, service agreements, and legal commitments.
