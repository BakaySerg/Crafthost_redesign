Protection of Crafthost’s Registered European Trademark.

Crafthost is a registered and protected trademark within the European Union. This designation ensures that the brand, its identity elements, and all associated service marks are legally safeguarded under EU intellectual property law. The purpose of this page is to clarify ownership rights, outline permitted usage, and define restrictions related to the Crafthost trademark.

This protection reflects Crafthost’s commitment to maintaining a trusted, authentic, and unified brand presence across Europe and the global markets in which we operate.

1. Trademark Ownership.

The name “Crafthost”, along with associated logos, marks, brand identifiers, and service names, is legally registered with:

Registered:
European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO)
Official Registration:
Crafthost SRL
Registered Status:
Active – Protected Under EU Trademark Law

2. Scope of Trademark Protection.

The Crafthost trademark covers all branding and identity elements used in relation to its products and services, including:

The Crafthost brand name.

Official Crafthost logos.

Service names and sub-brands.

Marks used in infrastructure, hosting, or digital services.

Promotional materials, badges, and icons.

Domain-based brand assets.

These elements cannot be copied, reproduced, or adapted without explicit written authorization.

3. Prohibited Use.

Any unauthorized use of the Crafthost trademark is strictly prohibited, including:

  • Using the Crafthost name or logo to imply endorsement
  • Reproducing branded visual assets in marketing or sales materials
  • Using similar variations or confusingly similar marks
  • Displaying Crafthost identifiers on third-party websites without permission
  • Integrating Crafthost trademarks into domain names or product brands

Crafthost actively monitors misuse and reserves full rights to enforce trademark protection through legal action when necessary.

4. Authorized or Permitted Use.

Organizations wishing to reference or use Crafthost’s trademarked materials must obtain written approval beforehand.
Authorized use includes:

  • Partner-related branding
  • Integration acknowledgements for certified vendors
  • Press releases or media publications citing Crafthost
  • Compliance-related documentation where required

All approved use must follow Crafthost’s brand and identity guidelines to ensure accuracy and consistency.

5. Legal Framework and Jurisdiction.

Crafthost’s trademark protection is governed by:

  • European Union Trademark Regulation (EUTMR)
  • European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO)
  • International IP treaties applicable to EU member states

Infringements may be pursued under EU trademark law, civil liability, and international intellectual property provisions as applicable.

6. Updates to This Trademark Statement.

This page may be revised to reflect changes in trademark status, legal requirements, or branding updates. Modifications take effect immediately upon publication.

Reporting Trademark Misuse.

Crafthost encourages the reporting of any suspected trademark infringement or unauthorized use. Verified reports support the integrity and legal protection of the brand.
Please include any relevant links, screenshots, or documentation to support the report.
