Managed Infrastructure Add-Ons.

Extend operational responsibility without expanding internal workload.

Crafthost Add-Ons are managed operational services that extend responsibility, control, and assurance across your infrastructure.

They are designed to complement Managed Cloud VPS, Managed Dedicated Servers, GPU & AI Infrastructure, and Enterprise Services.

Add-Ons are not infrastructure resources.
They do not replace core managed services.
They extend scope where higher assurance, compliance, or performance guarantees are required.

What These Add-Ons Are.

  • Engineer-managed operational extensions
  • SLA-aligned, responsibility-bound services
  • Applied selectively per environment, workload, or compliance scope
  • Integrated into existing managed service agreements

What These Add-Ons Are Not.

  • Feature toggles or self-service options
  • One-time setup tasks without ownership
  • Infrastructure pricing (servers, bandwidth, GPUs, storage)
  • Standalone services without managed operations

If you are seeking raw infrastructure upgrades, this page is not applicable.

Operational Continuity Add-Ons.

For environments where downtime, misconfiguration, or slow recovery carries measurable business impact.

  • Advanced Incident Response Coverage
    Extended escalation depth and senior engineer involvement for critical environments.
  • Change Governance & Release Oversight
    Controlled change execution, validation, rollback planning, and post-change review.
  • Disaster Recovery Coordination
    Engineer-managed recovery orchestration across defined recovery objectives.
  • Environment Standardization & Hardening
    Configuration baselining across multi-node or multi-region deployments.

Security & Risk Add-Ons.

For organizations operating under security, regulatory, or audit pressure.

  • Security Operations Augmentation (SecOps)
    Continuous security posture monitoring, alert triage, and response coordination.
  • Vulnerability Management Oversight
    Patch governance, exposure tracking, and remediation validation.
  • Incident Forensics & Post-Event Analysis
    Structured investigation and reporting after security or availability events.
  • Zero-Trust & Access Policy Enforcement
    Operational enforcement of access segmentation and control models.

Compliance & Governance Add-Ons.

For regulated industries and audit-driven environments.

  • Compliance Readiness Support
    Operational documentation aligned with ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, and sector-specific controls.
  • Audit Support & Evidence Preparation
    Engineer-assisted data preparation and audit coordination.
  • Policy Enforcement Monitoring
    Continuous verification that operational controls remain in effect.
  • Regulatory Change Impact Assessment
    Operational evaluation of new or updated compliance requirements.

Performance & Scalability Add-Ons.

For performance-sensitive or growth-driven workloads.

  • Proactive Performance Optimization
    Continuous tuning of system behavior under real workloads.
  • Capacity Planning & Forecasting
    Predictive scaling recommendations based on usage and growth patterns.
  • High-Availability Architecture Oversight
    Engineer-managed redundancy validation and failure-mode testing.
  • Workload-Aware Optimization
    Tailored operations for latency-sensitive, stateful, or compute-intensive systems.

Service Scope & Applicability.

Add-Ons may apply to:

  • Single environments
  • Selected production workloads
  • Multi-region or multi-vendor architectures
  • Regulated or business-critical systems only

Not all add-ons are applicable to all services. Scope is defined during assessment.
