Operational Knowledge for Production Infrastructure

The Crafthost Knowledge Base is a living operational reference designed for teams running real production infrastructure, not test environments.

It documents how systems behave, how incidents are handled, how changes are applied, and how responsibility is enforced across managed and self-managed platforms.

This is not generic help content.
It is engineering documentation written for accountability.

What the Knowledge Base Is — and Is Not

What It Is:
  • Operational procedures used by real engineers
  • Architecture-aware guidance
  • Incident and failure-oriented documentation
  • Designed for production, audit, and compliance contexts
  • Continuously updated as infrastructure evolves
What It Is Not:
  • Marketing documentation
  • Generic “how-to” tutorials
  • Entry-level cloud training
  • Vendor-agnostic abstraction
  • Feature explanation pages

Every article exists to reduce ambiguity during real operational moments.

How This Knowledge Base Is Maintained

  • Authored and reviewed by infrastructure engineers
  • Updated alongside platform changes
  • Aligned with real operational runbooks
  • Version-aware — no outdated assumptions

Articles evolve as architecture evolves.

Who This Knowledge Base Is For & Who It Is Not Designed For

Who This Knowledge Base Is For:
  • Production engineering teams
  • Platform and SRE leads
  • Security and compliance officers
  • Technical operations managers
  • Enterprise IT teams
Who It Is Not Designed For:
  • Hobby projects
  • Non-production experiments
  • Beginner cloud learners
  • Price-comparison audiences

This content assumes operational responsibility.

Using the Knowledge Base Effectively

Organizations typically use this knowledge base to:

  • Reduce incident response time
  • Standardize operational behavior
  • Train senior engineers
  • Support audits and compliance reviews
  • Align internal teams with external operators

Many customers treat it as an extension of their internal runbooks.
