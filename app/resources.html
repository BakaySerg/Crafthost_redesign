From Information to Infrastructure Decisions.

Turn Technical Knowledge into Better Architecture. Useful infrastructure content should support real decisions.

Crafthost Resources helps organizations evaluate questions such as:

  • Which infrastructure model best fits the workload?
  • Which operational responsibilities should remain internal?
  • Which responsibilities should be assigned to a managed infrastructure partner?
  • What level of isolation, control and performance is required?
  • How should security and governance influence architecture?
  • What availability and recovery requirements must the environment support?
  • What must be validated before migration?
  • How should capacity and future growth be planned?
  • When is specialized infrastructure justified?

These decisions affect more than technology selection.
They influence operational risk, staffing requirements, security, availability, performance and long-term infrastructure complexity. Crafthost approaches these questions from the perspective of both infrastructure engineering and ongoing operational responsibility.
