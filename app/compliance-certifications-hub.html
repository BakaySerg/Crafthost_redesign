GDPR Data Protection & Processing Compliance

Crafthost supports full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), ensuring that organizations handling EU personal data can operate in a secure, lawful, and transparent environment. Our GDPR framework is built on strict adherence to data protection principles and reinforced by robust technical and organizational measures.

Data collection and processing follow purpose-limitation, data-minimization, legal-basis alignment, and strict retention control. Personal information is safeguarded through encryption, access restrictions, continuous monitoring, and full lifecycle protection. Clear governance procedures define how data is handled, transferred, and stored across our European infrastructure.

Data subjects benefit from clear rights under GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, portability, and objection. Crafthost provides structured pathways to support these rights through documented procedures and secure verification steps. Each request is managed with precision to maintain regulatory alignment while protecting privacy and platform integrity.

Cross-border data transfers are protected through standardized contractual safeguards, such as SCCs, regional processing guarantees, and optional EU-only data residency models. Organizations retain complete control over how and where their data is processed.

Crafthost’s GDPR architecture ensures lawful processing, transparent operations, and strong privacy protection for organizations operating within Europe or serving EU residents.

Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

The Data Processing Agreement governs how Crafthost processes personal data on behalf of customers, providing clear definitions of responsibilities, roles, and security obligations. It reinforces legal compliance standards and ensures that all processing activities remain controlled, documented, and aligned with our regulatory commitments.

The DPA defines Crafthost as the processor and the customer as the controller. This relationship outlines the limits of processing, the purposes authorized by the customer, and the confidentiality expectations applied to all personnel involved in data operations. The agreement guarantees that data is handled in accordance with applicable laws, including GDPR where relevant.

Technical and organizational measures are embedded into the DPA, covering encryption, access control, logging, threat monitoring, resilience planning, and vulnerability management. These frameworks are reviewed and updated to reflect evolving security practices and regulatory requirements.

Sub-processors engaged by Crafthost undergo extensive due diligence to ensure they maintain equivalent levels of protection. Customers are notified of sub-processing activities and retain the right to raise objections or request additional safeguards when necessary.

The DPA empowers customers with clear rights and structured procedures, ensuring transparent data management across all services.

Global Security Overview

Crafthost’s security framework is designed for resilience, ensuring that global infrastructures remain protected against evolving threats while supporting the highest levels of operational continuity. A multi-layered security model safeguards data, applications, and networks across all regions.

Protection begins at the physical layer, with Tier-IV and Tier-V facilities featuring biometric controls, 24/7 monitoring, redundant power systems, and controlled access zones. Network-level security extends through advanced firewalls, DDoS mitigation, real-time intrusion detection, and encrypted connectivity for sensitive workloads.

Platform-wide security integrates threat intelligence, continuous monitoring, automated incident analysis, and hardened configurations. Encryption standards protect data in transit and at rest, ensuring confidentiality across all stages of the processing lifecycle.

Operational security frameworks establish strong governance, strict access controls, structured change management, and separation of responsibilities. Regular audits, penetration tests, and risk assessments validate the strength of every system and process.

This comprehensive model ensures that organizations operating in high-risk sectors can rely on stable, predictable, and secure infrastructures at all times.

Security Response Policy

Crafthost maintains a disciplined Security Response Policy that defines how incidents are detected, analyzed, escalated, and resolved. This structured approach supports rapid containment, transparent communication, and a minimized impact on customer operations.

Real-time monitoring systems track suspicious activity, abnormal patterns, and emerging threat indicators. Alerts are analyzed by specialized teams who categorize severity levels and initiate appropriate response actions. Each incident follows a coordinated workflow that includes investigation, mitigation, documentation, and verification. Customers benefit from structured notification procedures that maintain clarity and provide accurate, timely updates. Response actions prioritize the protection of data, restoration of services, and preservation of forensic evidence when required for audit or legal review.

Post-incident processes involve detailed analysis, corrective measures, and updates to the security framework to prevent recurrence. These improvements reinforce long-term resilience and maintain alignment with global compliance standards.

The Security Response Policy ensures that organizations receive dependable protection supported by disciplined operational governance.
