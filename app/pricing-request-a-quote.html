Request a Managed Infrastructure Quote.

This Is Not a Checkout Step.

Requesting a quote at Crafthost is not an instant pricing action and not a generic contact form.

It is the starting point for defining operational responsibility, risk boundaries, and engineering scope for production infrastructure environments.

This process exists to ensure that management, accountability, and cost structures are aligned correctly before any commercial proposal is issued.

What This Request Is For.

This request is designed for organizations that require engineer-led infrastructure operations, not unmanaged resources.

Typical use cases include:

  • Production Cloud VPS environments requiring ongoing operations
  • Dedicated servers with shared or unclear responsibility today
  • GPU & AI infrastructure supporting training or inference workloads
  • Regulated or risk-sensitive systems where uptime, security, and escalation paths matter

If you are unsure which service applies, submitting this request allows us to identify the correct path during scoping.

What Happens After You Submit.

Initial Review
  • Your request is reviewed by infrastructure engineer, not an automated system.
  • Response time: within 1–2 business days (Italy and EU requests are prioritized)
Technical Scoping (If Qualified)
  • Operational responsibility, risks, and dependencies are validated.
  • Typical duration: 5–10 business days, depending on complexity.
Commercial Proposal
  • A proposal is issued reflecting actual operational scope, not templated assumptions.

Who Should Use This Request.

This request is appropriate if:

  • Your organization operates production or business-critical infrastructure
  • Annual infrastructure investment typically exceeds €100,000
  • You require clear ownership, escalation paths, and accountability

Who Should Not Use This Request.

This request is not suitable for:

  • Hobby, test, or experimental workloads
  • Price-only comparisons without operational scope
  • Short-term or unmanaged resource needs

Requests that do not require managed operations are redirected early to avoid wasted time.

Privacy & Data Handling.

Quote requests are reviewed only by Crafthost engineers.
No information is shared with third parties or used for marketing purposes without explicit consent, in full alignment with EU GDPR requirements.
