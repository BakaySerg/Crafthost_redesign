Data Retention Policy.

Clear Guidelines for Data Lifecycle, Storage, and Secure Deletion.

Organisations operating in regulated sectors rely on predictable, transparent data handling. Crafthost maintains strict data-retention controls to ensure that every stage of the data lifecycle—collection, storage, archival, and deletion—aligns with GDPR requirements, contractual obligations, and security best practices. This policy outlines how long different categories of data are retained, how they are protected, and when they are securely removed.

Crafthost applies these standards across all services, including Managed Cloud VPS, Managed Dedicated Servers, Private Cloud, Hybrid Cloud, and GPU/AI infrastructures. Our retention approach supports operational continuity while ensuring compliance with international regulations and customer expectations.

1. Purpose of This Policy.

The Data Retention Policy ensures that:

  • Data is stored only for as long as necessary
  • Retention periods align with legal, regulatory, and operational requirements
  • Expired data is securely deleted or anonymised
  • Customers receive clear, consistent lifecycle transparency

This policy applies to all customer data processed or stored by Crafthost.

2. Categories of Data Covered.

Crafthost retains everal types of data associated with account operations and infrastructure services. Retention periods vary based on purpose and regulatory requirements.

A.

Account & Billing Information

Includes contact details, invoices, transaction records, and payment history.

B.

Service Configuration Data

Details related to provisioning, resource allocation, IP addresses, and system settings.

C.

Operational Logs

Metadata generated during system operations, such as access logs, audit logs, security events, and network performance details.

D.

Support Interactions

Tickets, email communications, and chat transcripts used for service continuity and quality assurance.

E.

Backup & Snapshot Data

Automated or manually created copies of customer environments held for disaster recovery.

F.

Compliance-Related Data

Information kept to satisfy legal obligations, disputes, fraud prevention, and audit requirements.

3. Standard Retention Periods.

Retention periods are designed to balance legal obligations, operational requirements, and customer expectations. Unless contractually modified, Crafthost follows the durations below.
All retention periods follow GDPR principles of storage limitation and purpose limitation.

A.

Account & Billing Information

  • Retention:
    up to 10 years.
  • Purpose:
    accounting, regulatory compliance, tax requirements.
B.

Service Configuration Data

  • Retention:
    duration of active service + 90 days after cancellation.
  • Purpose:
    restoration assistance, troubleshooting, dispute resolution.
C.

Operational & Security Logs

  • Retention:
    90 to 365 days depending on log type.
  • Purpose:
    security analysis, intrusion detection, performance optimisation, compliance.
D.

Support Tickets & Communications

  • Retention:
    up to 3 years.
  • Purpose:
    operational continuity, service quality assurance, audit trails.
E.

Backups & Snapshots

  • Retention:
    depends on selected service tier (typically 7–30 days).
  • Purpose:
    disaster recovery, system restoration, continuity.
F.

Compliance & Regulatory Data

  • Retention:
    as required by law or governing authority.
  • Purpose:
    fulfilment of contractual, legal, and regulatory duties.

4. Secure Data Deletion.

When data reaches the end of its retention period, Crafthost performs secure deletion using industry-standard practices:

  • Cryptographic erasure for encrypted data
  • Overwrite or secure wipe where applicable
  • Removal from all active and standby systems
  • Deletion across backup, replication, and archival layers

Deleted data cannot be reconstructed and is permanently destroyed.
Customers may request earlier deletion where legally permitted.

5. Customer-Controlled Retention Options.

Some services allow customers to control their own data-retention periods.
This includes:

  • Configurable backup schedules
  • Custom snapshot retention windows
  • Log storage extensions for enterprise plans

Customers who require extended retention for compliance (finance, healthcare, government) may request a service-level modification.

6. Exceptional Retention Circumstances.

Crafthost may retain certain data longer if required for:

  • Fraud prevention investigations
  • Ongoing disputes or chargebacks
  • Incident-response documentation
  • Compliance with court orders or regulatory mandates

Retention is always limited to what is strictly necessary.

7. Customer Data Portability.

In compliance with GDPR, customers have the right to request:

  • Export of personal data
  • Structured machine-readable delivery
  • Transfer to another provider

Requests are handled securely and in accordance with authentication procedures.

8. Relationship to GDPR, DPA & Subprocessors.

This policy complements:

  • GDPR Data Protection Hub
  • Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
  • Subprocessors List
  • Global Security Overview

Together, these documents provide a complete transparency framework.

9. Updates to This Policy.

Crafthost may update retention schedules when:

  • Regulations change
  • Security requirements evolve
  • New services are introduced

Updates are reflected on this page and communicated to enterprise customers when relevant.
