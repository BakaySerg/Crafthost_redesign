Service Cancellation Policy.

Clear, Fair, and Transparent Guidelines for Ending or Adjusting Your Crafthost Services.

Purpose of This Policy General Cancellation Principles How to Request Cancellation Notice Period Requirements Billing Cycle and Financial Obligations Contracts, Commitments, and Term Plans Data Retention and Removal Impact on Associated Services Upgrades, Downgrades, and Replacements Suspension vs. Cancellation Account Closure Exceptional Circumstances Our Commitment to a Smooth Transition Final Notes

Purpose of This Policy.

The Service Cancellation Policy explains how customers may cancel, downgrade, or modify their Crafthost services. It provides clarity on notice periods, billing cycles, data handling, and the steps required to ensure a smooth and disruption-free transition.

The goal is to offer a transparent, predictable process that protects both operational continuity and customer interests. This policy applies to all core services, including Managed Cloud VPS, Managed Dedicated Servers, Private Cloud, Hybrid Cloud, GPU/AI compute, and enterprise solutions.

General Cancellation Principles.

Crafthost allows service cancellation at any time according to the terms outlined in the client’s account and agreement. All cancellations must be submitted through an authorized support channel to ensure proper verification and traceability.

The cancellation date is aligned with the current billing cycle unless otherwise stated in a dedicated contract.Customers retain full control over service termination timelines to avoid unexpected interruptions.
Clear records of cancellation requests are preserved for audit, compliance, and operational accuracy.

How to Request Cancellation.

Cancellation requests must be submitted via the official support portal or through the primary account holder’s authenticated dashboard. Requests should include the service ID, account email, and the preferred cancellation date.

Crafthost reviews every request to confirm identity, verify contractual terms, and prevent accidental or unauthorized termination. Once validated, confirmation is issued along with the effective cancellation date. A final summary is provided outlining billing status, data retention options, and offboarding steps.

Notice Period Requirements.

Most services follow a standard notice requirement linked to the billing cycle.Cancellations submitted before the next renewal date apply from the upcoming cycle. br Enterprise contracts or custom infrastructure agreements may include specific notice periods defined in the individual SLA.

These requirements ensure orderly resource release, migration support, and platform stability.Customers are encouraged to initiate cancellations early when planning large or complex transitions.

Billing Cycle and Financial Obligations.

Crafthost operates on prepaid billing cycles.
Services remain active until the end of the cycle in which the cancellation request is submitted and confirmed. No partial refunds are offered for unused days within the billing period unless explicitly stated in a separate agreement.

Outstanding balances or add-on costs must be settled before account closure. Upon successful cancellation, no future invoices for the terminated service will be generated.

Contracts, Commitments, and Term Plans.

If a service is under a monthly, annual, or multi-year contract, cancellations must follow the terms in the agreement. Early termination may incur fees if documented within contractual conditions.

Crafthost ensures all contract terms are communicated clearly during onboarding and renewal. Customers may request a contract review or upgrade instead of cancelling, depending on operational needs. These structured terms protect both parties from unexpected operational risk.

Data Retention and Removal.

After cancellation, customer data is retained for a short grace period to prevent accidental loss.
This retention period varies depending on the service type and compliance requirements.

Once the retention window ends, all remaining data is securely deleted using strict data-destruction protocols. Customers are responsible for exporting or backing up their data before termination. Crafthost can provide migration assistance if requested before the cancellation takes effect.

Impact on Associated Services.

Cancelling one service may affect related or dependent services, such as backups, IP addresses, firewall rules, or private interconnects.

Customers should review dependencies before submitting cancellation requests. Support teams can advise on how to maintain continuity if other services must remain active. Failure to address dependencies may result in unexpected configuration changes or data unavailability. Proactive planning ensures seamless infrastructure stability.

Upgrades, Downgrades, and Replacements.

Before cancelling, customers may choose to downgrade or upgrade instead of terminating the service.
Crafthost offers flexible alternatives including higher-capacity dedicated servers, smaller VPS plans, or hybrid cloud replacements.

In many cases, adjusting the service is more efficient than cancelling and re-ordering. Support teams can guide customers through comparisons to select the best operational match. This approach can reduce downtime and maintain critical workloads.

Suspension vs. Cancellation.

Service suspension differs from cancellation.
Suspension typically occurs due to overdue payments, AUP violations, or security incidents. During suspension, the service remains active but inaccessible until the issue is resolved. Cancellation is a final termination that triggers the shutdown process and data removal. Understanding the distinction helps customers manage their accounts responsibly.

Account Closure.

Cancellation of individual services does not automatically close the entire customer account. A separate request is required for full account termination. All active services must be cancelled and settled before the account can be closed.

Crafthost maintains archived billing and verification records for compliance purposes. Account closure is confirmed only when all policies and financial obligations are fulfilled.

Exceptional Circumstances.

Some cancellations may occur under exceptional conditions such as platform migration, regulatory requirements, or contractual changes.

In these cases, Crafthost provides clear notices and structured transition support. Customers are informed in advance of any material modifications to their services. Exceptional circumstances are handled with transparency and minimal disruption. This ensures uninterrupted operations for mission-critical environments.

Our Commitment to a Smooth Transition.

Crafthost values long-term relationships and ensures every cancellation is handled with professionalism and respect.

Support teams assist customers in preserving continuity, retrieving data, and migrating workloads if needed. The process is structured to maintain operational integrity and protect customer interests. Customers are guided through every step to avoid downtime or data loss. This commitment reflects the reliability and trust that define Crafthost.

Final Notes.

This Service Cancellation Policy is designed to provide clarity, fairness, and predictability. It complements the official Terms of Service, Billing Policy, and Data Protection frameworks.

Customers are encouraged to review the linked documents for full governance detail. Understanding this policy ensures informed decision-making and a stable service lifecycle. Clear procedures support both customer success and infrastructure reliability.

Crafthost provides managed cloud, dedicated, and intelligent IaaS services operated by experienced engineers, designed for organizations running production-critical workloads.
