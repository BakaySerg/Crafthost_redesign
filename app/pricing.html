How Crafthost Pricing Works.

Crafthost pricing is structured around who operates the infrastructure, who responds to incidents, and who is accountable for outcomes.

To maintain clarity and prevent scope ambiguity, pricing is delivered through distinct commercial paths, each aligned to a defined responsibility model.

Crafthost does not compete on the lowest price; we compete on operational certainty, accountability, and predictable outcomes.

Infrastructure.

Self-Managed Resources

Infrastructure pricing applies to organizations that operate and govern their environments independently, without managed operations.

This model supports flexibility and scale for experienced technical teams while remaining clearly separated from managed and enterprise responsibility models.

Pricing Reflects Risk & Responsibility.

Enterprise pricing is determined by who carries the operational and business risk.

Pricing is shaped by six core considerations:

  • Operational responsibility and governance scope
  • Service level objectives and response commitments
  • Regulatory and compliance requirements
  • Architectural complexity and system topology
  • Geographic distribution and performance targets
  • Business impact associated with service disruption

Enterprise pricing is non-linear by design.
As operational responsibility increases, pricing reflects the risk Crafthost assumes on your behalf.
