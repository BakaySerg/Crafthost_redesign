Law Enforcement Request Policy.

Law Enforcement Our Guiding Principles Types of Requests Accepted Requirements for Valid Requests Customer Data Under Crafthost Control Customer Notification International Requests Emergency Requests Data Preservation Requests Rejection of Improper or Unlawful Requests Transparency Reporting

Last Updated: 2025

This Law Enforcement Request Policy explains how Crafthost SRL (“Crafthost”) handles requests from government authorities, regulatory bodies, and law enforcement agencies seeking access to customer information or hosted data.

Crafthost is committed to protecting customer privacy while complying with applicable laws in the jurisdictions where it operates. This policy applies to all Crafthost services, including Managed Hosting, Managed Cloud & VPS, Dedicated Servers, GPU & AI infrastructure, Private Cloud, and all managed enterprise solutions.

1. Our Guiding Principles

Crafthost follows these principles when responding to law-enforcement requests:

Legality

Every request must be legally valid and issued under applicable law.

Customer Privacy

Customer rights and confidentiality are protected to the highest possible degree.

Minimal Disclosure

Only the minimum necessary information is disclosed.

Transparency

When allowed by law, customers are notified before disclosure.

Security

Data is handled securely throughout the request process.

2. Types of Requests Accepted

Crafthost processes only the following types of requests:

/

Court Orders / Warrants

Issued by a competent court or judicial authority requiring disclosure of specific data.

/

Subpoenas / Administrative Orders

Legally binding requests issued under national or regional laws.

/

Emergency Disclosure Requests

Only in situations involving:

  • Imminent danger to life
  • Serious physical harm
  • Or urgent threats to critical infrastructure
/

Requests Under International Cooperation Treaties

Requests submitted through MLATs (Mutual Legal Assistance Treaties) or recognized international channels.

3. Requirements for Valid Requests

To process a request, the following must be clearly provided:

  • Name of requesting agency
  • Legal authority and basis for the request
  • Specific data being sought
  • Time period of the data
  • Contact details of the authorized officer
  • Official signature and agency documentation
  • Relevant case number or legal reference

Crafthost rejects incomplete or unauthorized requests.

4. Customer Data Under Crafthost Control

Crafthost maintains two categories of data:

Account Information (Under Crafthost Control)
  • Account owner details
  • Billing data
  • Contact information
  • Service plan usage
  • Logs related to access and operational security
Customer Content (Customer-Controlled Data)

This includes anything stored, transmitted, or created by the customer inside servers or cloud environments.

Crafthost does not access, modify, or monitor customer content unless explicitly instructed by the customer or required by law.

5. Customer Notification

Unless prohibited by law (e.g., gag orders), Crafthost notifies the customer before disclosing any information.
Notification includes:

  • A copy of the request
  • The data requested
  • Notification of compliance deadline
  • Opportunity for the customer to challenge the request

If a legal prohibition exists, notification occurs once the restriction is lifted.

6. International Requests

For agencies outside the European Union or the jurisdiction where the data is stored:

  • Requests must follow international cooperation channels
  • Direct requests from foreign law enforcement are not accepted without proper treaties or supervisory review
  • Data stored in the EU is protected under GDPR and the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union

Crafthost ensures that transfers comply with international data protection laws.

7. Emergency Requests

Handled exclusively by the Security & Compliance Team.
The request must include:

  • Explanation of the emergency
  • Nature of imminent harm
  • Legal authority of the requester
  • Identity verification of the officer

If the request is verified and meets emergency criteria, minimal necessary data may be disclosed.

8. Data Preservation Requests

Upon receiving a valid preservation request from law enforcement:

  • Crafthost preserves the requested data for up to 90 days, extendable upon legal authority
  • Data is not disclosed unless a proper legal request follows

9. Rejection of Improper or Unlawful Requests

Crafthost rejects requests that are:

  • Vague or overly broad
  • Not legally valid
  • Outside the jurisdiction or lacking authority
  • Missing required documentation
  • Violating customer rights or international data protection laws

Where appropriate, the agency will be informed of the reasons for rejection.

10. Transparency Reporting

Crafthost may periodically publish aggregated, non-identifiable statistics regarding law-enforcement requests, consistent with regulatory guidelines.

Contact Information for Law Enforcement.

Requests submitted through customer support channels are not processed.
