Enterprise Infrastructure Migration Services.

Zero-Downtime Transitions for Mission-Critical Systems.

We plan, execute, and validate complex migrations across cloud, dedicated, hybrid, and sovereign environments—while maintaining service continuity, security posture, and compliance obligations.

This is not lift-and-shift automation.This is operator-led migration with accountability for outcomes.

Migration Challenges We Eliminate.

Most infrastructure migrations fail not because of technology—but because of poor ownership boundaries.

Crafthost eliminates common failure points:

  • Downtime during cutover
  • Configuration drift and hidden dependencies
  • Data inconsistency or replication lag
  • Security regressions and compliance gaps
  • Vendor lock-in assumptions
  • Undefined rollback responsibility

We assume operational accountability, not advisory distance.

Our Migration Methodology.

Architecture & Risk Assessment.

We begin with a structured technical and operational assessment:

  • Current-state architecture mapping
  • Dependency and traffic flow analysis
  • Compliance, data residency, and regulatory constraints
  • RTO / RPO and business impact modeling

Migration Blueprint.

A migration plan is produced and validated before any action:

  • Target architecture design
  • Migration sequencing and timelines
  • Cutover strategy (zero-downtime or controlled window)
  • Rollback and fail-safe procedures
  • Responsibility matrix (Crafthost vs client vs third parties)

No execution begins without sign-off.

Staged Execution.

Migrations are executed in controlled phases:

  • Parallel environment provisioning
  • Secure data replication and validation
  • Incremental workload transition
  • Performance and integrity verification
  • Final cutover under active monitoring

Post-Migration Stabilization.

After cutover, we remain accountable:

  • Performance tuning and optimization
  • Security posture validation
  • Monitoring and alert calibration
  • Documentation and handover (if required)

Supported Target Platforms.

Migration services integrate directly with Crafthost solutions:

  • Managed Cloud VPS
  • Managed Dedicated Servers
  • High-Performance & GPU Infrastructure
  • Private Cloud
  • Hybrid & Sovereign Cloud Architectures

Compliance & Security by Design.

Migrations are executed with security and compliance embedded—not added later.

  • GDPR-aligned data handling
  • Industry-specific compliance alignment (finance, healthcare, government)
  • Encrypted data transfer and access control
  • Audit-ready documentation

Who This Solution Is Not For.

To avoid misalignment:

  • Not a self-service migration tool
  • Not a generic “lift & shift” script
  • Not a fixed-price commodity offering
  • Not limited to Crafthost-only infrastructure
  • Not suitable for non-critical or hobby workloads

Who This Is For.

  • Enterprises operating mission-critical systems
  • Organizations with regulatory or data-sovereignty constraints
  • Businesses consolidating or modernizing complex infrastructure
  • Teams requiring clear accountability during transition
