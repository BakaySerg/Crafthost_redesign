User Agreement Summary.

Clear Guidelines for Using Crafthost Services Responsibly and Effectively.

Purpose of This Summary.

This User Agreement Summary provides a simplified explanation of the core terms governing your use of Crafthost services. It highlights the key commitments, responsibilities, rights, and expectations that apply when engaging with our infrastructure.

The aim is to ensure users understand their obligations without navigating complex legal language.
For full legal detail, users should review the official Terms of Service.
This page functions as a practical, quick-reference guide.

Scope of Services.

Crafthost provides managed hosting, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise-grade compute services tailored to SMEs and large organisations. Services may include Managed Cloud VPS, Managed Dedicated Servers, Private and Hybrid Cloud environments, GPU and AI compute systems, and Migration Services.
All offerings operate within regulated frameworks aligned with data security, uptime standards, and performance requirements.

Users must engage with each service according to the technical and operational parameters defined by Crafthost. These parameters ensure continuity, predictability, and stable long-term performance.

User Responsibilities.

Users are responsible for ensuring their content, applications, and operational activities comply with applicable laws and industry standards. This includes—but is not limited to—copyright rules, data protection laws, security policies, and acceptable use guidelines.

Customers must configure their systems responsibly and maintain appropriate safeguards to protect their data and credentials. They must avoid any activity that may harm platform integrity, disrupt network stability, or infringe upon the rights of others. Failure to meet these responsibilities may result in access restrictions or service suspension.

Crafthost’s Responsibilities.

Crafthost is committed to delivering stable, secure, and high-performance infrastructure built for mission-critical operations. We maintain robust environments based on Tier-IV and Tier-V principles, multi-layered security controls, and continuous monitoring.

Our teams support users through structured service levels, defined response timelines, and advanced protective measures. We uphold operational transparency, maintain clear communication channels, and enforce strong compliance frameworks. These commitments ensure reliable service continuity across global regions.

Account Security & Access.

Users must protect account credentials, API keys, and administrative access points with strong security practices. Crafthost provides secure authentication methods, access logs, and optional advanced protections to support safe usage.

Any unauthorized account activity must be reported immediately for rapid investigation.
Users are responsible for internal management of team members, subcontractors, or external parties accessing their environment. Protecting access is essential for maintaining operational integrity.

Acceptable Use Requirements.

All users must follow the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP), which outlines prohibited activities such as illegal content distribution, network abuse, or harmful automation.

Infrastructure may not be used to perform actions that compromise other systems, networks, or individuals. High-risk activities require prior approval to ensure safe execution within controlled environments. Crafthost enforces these rules to maintain platform stability and regulatory alignment. Violation of the AUP may result in restricted access or permanent service termination.

Billing & Service Payments.

Crafthost services follow transparent billing cycles and pricing structures defined on the official Pricing pages.
Users must maintain updated payment information and ensure timely settlement of invoices.

Failure to pay may lead to temporary service suspension until the balance is resolved. Refunds, credits, or adjustments follow the policies outlined in the Billing and Refund Policy. Predictable billing supports smooth planning and uninterrupted operations.

Data Protection & Privacy.

Crafthost implements strict data protection principles aligned with GDPR and other international regulations.User data remains confidential and is processed only as necessary to deliver core services.

Customers maintain full ownership of their data while Crafthost ensures secure handling, storage, and transmission. Data processing activities follow transparent rules outlined in the Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement (DPA). These protections ensure trust, confidentiality, and regulatory compliance.

Content Ownership & Intellectual Property.

Users retain complete ownership of the content, systems, and applications hosted on the platform.
Crafthost does not claim rights over user-generated or user-managed materials. However, users must ensure that their content does not infringe upon intellectual property laws or third-party rights.

Crafthost responds promptly to valid copyright concerns and DMCA notices.Respect for intellectual property helps maintain a lawful and resilient digital ecosystem.

Service Modifications & Updates.

Crafthost may improve or update its infrastructure, security measures, or service offerings to maintain optimal performance.

Changes are designed to strengthen reliability, efficiency, and user experience across all services. Users will be informed of updates that significantly impact operational workflows or technical requirements. Routine enhancements may be applied without prior notice to support continuous improvement. This evolution ensures the platform remains aligned with modern technological standards.

Suspension or Termination of Services.

Crafthost may suspend or terminate access in cases of unpaid invoices, policy violations, unlawful activity, or security threats.

We balance enforcement with fairness by offering clear notices and reasonable remediation periods. Users may also request voluntary termination or plan adjustments through designated channels. Data handling and retention timelines follow documented offboarding procedures. These safeguards protect both customers and the platform from operational risks.

Limitation of Liability.

Crafthost provides enterprise-grade infrastructure engineered for reliability and continuity. However, certain limitations apply as outlined in the full Terms of Service. Users should review those clauses to understand boundaries related to performance, damages, and third-party risks.

These limitations ensure fair allocation of responsibility within complex technical ecosystems.
They also align the platform with industry standards for managed infrastructure providers.

Dispute Resolution.

Crafthost aims to resolve concerns through structured communication and transparent processes. Formal dispute mechanisms follow the guidelines set out in the Terms of Service.

Users may escalate unresolved matters to designated channels for further evaluation. These pathways ensure fairness, clarity, and timely resolution. Our process prioritises constructive outcomes and sustained trust.

Reference to Full Terms.

This User Agreement Summary highlights the main elements governing service usage. It does not replace the full Terms of Service, which contain complete legal details.

All users are expected to review the full Terms before engaging with Crafthost infrastructure. The summary exists to make obligations easier to understand and follow. Clear expectations support strong partnerships and predictable operations.

Commitment to Transparency & Trust.

Crafthost was built for organisations that depend on stable, secure, and compliant digital environments. This summary reflects our dedication to transparency, responsibility, and professional governance.

By providing accessible guidance, we support informed decision-making and long-term operational confidence. Strong policy frameworks ensure protection for users, partners, and the platform itself. These principles form the foundation of every service we provide.

Reporting Trademark Misuse.

Crafthost encourages the reporting of any suspected trademark infringement or unauthorized use. Verified reports support the integrity and legal protection of the brand.
Please include any relevant links, screenshots, or documentation to support the report.
