Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI).

We welcome talent from all backgrounds, identities, and experiences. Crafthost is committed to creating a workplace where everyone feels safe, respected, and empowered.

Our DEI Commitment:

  • We encourage applicants who identify as women, underrepresented minorities, LGBTQ+ individuals, and people with disabilities.
  • We provide accommodations at any step of the hiring process and throughout employment.
  • We foster a culture built on psychological safety, inclusion, and equal opportunity.
  • We support future Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) aligned with cultural heritage, women in tech, accessibility, mental wellness, and more.
