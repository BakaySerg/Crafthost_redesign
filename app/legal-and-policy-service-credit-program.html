Service Credit Program.

Rewarding Reliability, Supporting Continuity, and Ensuring Fair Value for Every Customer.

Purpose of the Service Credit Program.

The Service Credit Program provides a structured, transparent way for customers to receive service credits when uptime or performance commitments fall below the standards defined in the Service Level Agreement (SLA).

Its purpose is to reinforce trust, ensure accountability, and demonstrate our commitment to uninterrupted operations across all regions and service categories. This program applies to Managed Cloud VPS, Managed Dedicated Servers, Private Cloud, Hybrid Cloud, GPU/AI compute, enterprise security services, and all related infrastructure layers covered under the SLA.

Our Commitment to Uptime and Performance.

Crafthost allows service cancellation at any time according to the terms outlined in the client’s account and agreement. All cancellations must be submitted through an authorized support channel to ensure proper verification and traceability.

The cancellation date is aligned with the current billing cycle unless otherwise stated in a dedicated contract.Customers retain full control over service termination timelines to avoid unexpected interruptions.
Clear records of cancellation requests are preserved for audit, compliance, and operational accuracy.

Eligibility for Service Credits.

Customers may qualify for credits when service availability drops below the SLA thresholds applicable to their plan.

Eligibility is tied to monthly uptime calculations for each specific service instance. Resource-level disruptions within the customer’s control are excluded from eligibility. Incidents must be verified, documented, and validated through support channels. All credits apply to future invoices and cannot be exchanged for monetary refunds.

Uptime Thresholds and Applicable Service Credits.

Service credit percentages are based on measured availability during a billing cycle. The thresholds below represent typical structures applied across major infrastructure providers, ensuring fairness and industry alignment.

Credit values apply per affected service and correspond strictly to the measured uptime.

Availability Ranges

99.0% – 99.9% Availability

Customers receive a service credit equivalent to a percentage of the monthly fee for the affected service.

95.0% – 98.99% Availability

A higher credit tier applies due to more significant impact on operations within the billing cycle.

Below 95% Availability

Maximum credit tiers apply to recognize the severity of downtime and disruption.

Sustained or repeated incidents

May qualify for escalated credit tiers or temporary fee adjustments under extended review.

How to Request Service Credits.

Crafthost follows a transparent and efficient process for credit requests. Customers must submit a support ticket within a defined window following the incident.

The request should include detailed timestamps and summaries of observed service impact. Crafthost verifies each request using system logs, monitoring tools, and uptime telemetry. Valid requests are processed promptly, with credits applied to the next billing cycle.

Timeframe for Requests.

Credit requests must be submitted within the timeframe specified in the SLA. Failure to submit within the designated period may render the request ineligible.

Crafthost ensures that each verified request is fully honored according to policy. This timeframe supports accurate log verification and timely credit issuance. Customers receive written confirmation once processing is complete.

Limitations and Exclusions.

Certain circumstances fall outside the scope of the Service Credit Program. Credits are not issued for outages caused by customer configurations or unmanaged applications.

Scheduled maintenance windows announced in advance do not qualify for service credits. Events beyond reasonable control, such as force majeure conditions, are excluded from eligibility. Credits cannot be stacked or exchanged for cash, nor applied to third-party fees or add-ons.

How Service Credits Are Applied.

Credits are issued as reductions on future invoices for the affected service. They do not apply retroactively to past invoices already billed.

Credits appear as line items for full transparency on the following billing cycle. Multiple credits are merged when applicable but capped according to SLA-defined limits. All credits remain valid only while the customer account is in good standing.

Continuous Improvement and Transparency.

We continuously refine availability standards and monitoring practices to reduce downtime risks. Metrics and incident patterns are reviewed to strengthen system architecture and preventive measures.

Customers benefit from global redundancy, predictive monitoring, and rapid response mechanisms. All improvements contribute to reduced impact and higher uptime consistency. Transparency remains central to how credits are evaluated and issued.

Benefits of the Service Credit Program.

The program ensures that customers experience fairness and structured compensation during downtime. It supports long-term trust by holding the platform accountable to high-availability standards. Insured compensation helps organizations maintain budget stability during outages.

This approach reinforces the value of choosing a provider with enterprise-grade architecture. Crafthost remains committed to supporting operations through clarity, reliability, and accountability.

Final Statement.

The Service Credit Program represents Crafthost’s commitment to long-term operational integrity.It ensures that customers receive fair value and predictable compensation whenever service availability falls below promised standards.

This structured, transparent model strengthens trust and reinforces the performance guarantees that define our infrastructure. Customers can operate with confidence, knowing that their investment is protected by one of the industry's most reliable credit frameworks. Crafthost continues to stand behind its platform through clarity, accountability, and enduring commitment to excellence.
