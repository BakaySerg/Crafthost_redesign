What This Estimate Represents.

This indicative range reflects:
  • Operational responsibility depth
  • Response expectations
  • Engineering involvement level
  • Governance and compliance overhead
It does not represent:
  • Infrastructure costs
  • Hardware or cloud resource fees
  • Licensing or third-party services

When to Proceed to Assessment.

You should proceed to a formal assessment if:

  • Your environment supports production workloads
  • Downtime, security incidents, or performance degradation represent business risk
  • You require a single accountable operator for infrastructure operations

Next Step — Begin Infrastructure Assessment.

A formal assessment converts this estimate into a precise, contract-ready scope.

What happens next:

  • Initial review within 1–2 business days (Italy & EU priority)
  • Technical scoping conducted by infrastructure engineers
  • Clear responsibility boundaries and SLA alignment
  • Final commercial proposal based on actual operational needs

Important Notes.

  • This calculator is non-binding
  • Submitting an assessment request does not obligate you to proceed
  • No payment or commitment is required
  • The goal is clarity before cost

When infrastructure operations affect revenue, compliance, or customer trust, the right decision is not guessing costs — it is defining responsibility.
This calculator exists to help you decide whether that step is warranted.
