DMCA Policy.

Protecting Intellectual Property With Fairness, Integrity, and Transparency.

Compliance With Copyright Law

Crafthost complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and other international copyright laws. We recognise the rights of creators, publishers, software developers, and content owners to protect their intellectual property.

This policy exists to ensure that legitimate infringement claims are handled promptly, accurately, and transparently.

Crafthost does not tolerate intentional misuse of copyrighted materials within hosted environments.We act responsibly to uphold lawful content practices across the platform.

Customer Responsibility for Content

Customers retain full responsibility for all content they upload, store, distribute, or manage through Crafthost services.

This includes website content, applications, databases, media files, documents, and any material transmitted via customer systems. Customers must ensure that all content respects copyright law and does not infringe upon the rights of third parties.

Crafthost does not pre-screen or monitor customer content, but we do respond to valid infringement notices. Failure to comply with copyright rules may result in content removal or account action.

Submitting a Valid DMCA Takedown Notice

If you believe your copyrighted material is being used without authorization on Crafthost services, you may submit a formal DMCA Takedown Notice.
A valid notice must include the following elements:

/

Identification of the copyrighted work

Provide adequate detail or representative examples of the protected material.

/

Identification of the infringing content

Include precise URLs, links, or locations within the hosted environment.

/

Good-faith statement

Confirm that the use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

/

Statement of accuracy

Declare, under penalty of perjury, that the information provided is accurate.

/

Your contact information

Include name, organisation (if applicable), address, email, and telephone number.

/

Physical or electronic signature

Submit a signature verifying your identity and authority to issue the notice.

Incomplete DMCA notices may be rejected or delayed until corrected.

Crafthost’s Response to DMCA Notices

Once a valid DMCA notice is received, Crafthost follows this structured process:

Acknowledgment
We confirm receipt of the notice and begin the review process.

Verification
We evaluate the claim and verify that all required elements are present.

Notification to Customer
We inform the customer hosting the allegedly infringing content, providing them the opportunity to respond.

Content Removal or Restriction
In cases where infringement appears likely, Crafthost may remove, disable, or restrict access to the content.

Review and Documentation
We maintain internal records of the notice, actions taken, and any follow-up correspondence.

Crafthost does not remove content unless a claim meets the criteria for a valid DMCA request.

Submitting a Counter-Notice

Customers who believe a takedown was issued in error may submit a formal DMCA Counter-Notice.
A valid counter-notice must include:

/

Identification of removed content

Specify what content was removed and its previous location.

/

Statement of good-faith belief

Confirm belief that the content was removed due to mistake or misidentification.

/

Statement of jurisdiction

Agree to the applicable legal jurisdiction for resolving the claim.

/

Contact information

Include full name, organisation (if applicable), address, phone, and email.

/

Physical or electronic signature

Verify the accuracy and authenticity of the counter-notice.

Incomplete DMCA notices may be rejected or delayed until corrected.

Repeat Infringement Policy

Crafthost enforces a strict policy for instances of repeated or intentional copyright violations.
Customers who repeatedly host infringing content may face:

  • Temporary suspension
  • Permanent account restrictions
  • Termination of services
  • Removal of hosted data (in accordance with retention rules)

These steps protect the platform and its users from legal and operational risks.

Misuse or Fraudulent Claims

Crafthost takes misuse of the DMCA process seriously.
Knowingly filing false infringement claims can result in legal consequences under applicable copyright laws.
We may deny or reject notices that appear abusive, incomplete, or submitted without legitimate legal basis.

This ensures fairness for both copyright holders and customers operating in good faith. Rights holders must ensure that claims are submitted responsibly and accurately.

Preservation of Service Integrity

Crafthost aims to resolve DMCA matters efficiently while maintaining stable, uninterrupted service for legitimate customers.

We balance copyright enforcement with operational continuity to avoid unnecessary downtime or disruption. Content is removed only when required by law or when infringement is clearly demonstrated.

These procedures support a fair and predictable operational environment. Our goal is to maintain trust, clarity, and protection for all parties involved.

Updates to This Policy

Crafthost may update the DMCA Policy periodically to reflect legal changes, industry standards, or internal governance updates.

Revisions become effective immediately upon publication with the updated effective date. Customers and rights holders are encouraged to review this page regularly.

Continuous updates ensure compliance with evolving copyright laws and digital practices. This commitment reinforces long-term operational integrity.

Commitment to Intellectual Property Protection

Crafthost supports a digital ecosystem built on responsibility, fairness, and respect for creative rights.
This DMCA Policy reflects our dedication to protecting intellectual property while ensuring stable operations across all hosted environments.

By providing clear procedures, transparent communication, and structured enforcement, we help maintain a trusted platform for organisations around the world.

Respect for copyright is essential not only for legal compliance, but for the integrity of the digital landscape itself.
