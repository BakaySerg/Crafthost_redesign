FAQ

Is this managed hosting?

No—this page is self-managed infrastructure only.

How fast is provisioning?

Most environments deploy in minutes after checkout.

Can I upgrade to managed services later?

Yes—your infrastructure can transition to managed operations when needed.

What billing cycles are available?

Monthly and longer-term cycles depending on resource type.

What payment methods are supported?

Credit card and SEPA (EU) where available.

Is workload uptime guaranteed?

No—platform availability is covered; workload uptime depends on your design and operations.
