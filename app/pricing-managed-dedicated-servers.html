Managed Dedicated Servers.

When Physical Infrastructure Ownership Demands Operational Accountability.

Enterprise infrastructure failures are rarely caused by insufficient hardware.They occur when operational responsibility is split—between hosting providers, internal teams, and external vendors—leaving no single party accountable when issues arise.

For organizations where performance consistency, data locality, compliance posture, and physical control directly affect business continuity and regulatory exposure, unmanaged or partially managed dedicated servers introduce unnecessary operational risk.

Managed Dedicated Servers by Crafthost are designed for organizations that require exclusive hardware ownership combined with engineer-led, SLA-aligned operations—without internal operational overhead.

What “Managed Dedicated” Means at Crafthost.

Managed Dedicated Servers shift day-to-day operational responsibility from fragmented internal teams and hosting vendors to a single accountable operator.

Crafthost operates your dedicated infrastructure as an externalized infrastructure operations function, contractually responsible for availability, security posture, and operational execution.

This service is not about server provisioning. It is about ownership of outcomes.

Managed Services Scope.

Operational Ownership

Full responsibility for server operations, escalation, and lifecycle management.

Hardware-Aware Operations

OS, firmware, performance, and failure domains managed with physical context.

SLA-Aligned Accountability

Service assurance governed by defined response and resolution commitments.

Single Escalation Path

No vendor coordination, no ambiguity, no handoffs.

What This Service Is — & Is Not.

This service is for organizations that:
  • Operate stateful or performance-sensitive workloads
  • Require physical hardware ownership or data residency
  • Run regulated, audited, or revenue-critical systems
  • Prefer accountability over internal operations staffing
This service is not for:
  • Self-service bare metal users
  • Cost-optimized or burst workloads
  • Organizations without a need for physical infrastructure control

If physical infrastructure ownership is not required, this service is excessive.
Managed Cloud VPS may be a better fit.

Pricing Philosophy.

Pricing Scales with Responsibility, Not Hardware Size Managed Dedicated pricing reflects operational complexity and responsibility scope, not CPU cores or RAM.

Factors determine engagement level:

  • Operational responsibility and escalation scope
  • Service level objectives and response commitments
  • Security and compliance requirements
  • Hardware topology and redundancy design
  • Geographic distribution and latency sensitivity
  • Business impact of downtime or degradation

Pricing is intentionally structured to support predictable, accountable operations, not commodity comparisons.

Managed Services vs Infrastructure Billing.

Important Cost Clarification

Managed Dedicated Server pricing covers operational services only.

Dedicated server hardware and colocation costs are billed separately by the infrastructure provider.

Example:
Managed Dedicated Services from €1,200 / month per server. Dedicated hardware & facility costs (provider-billed).

This separation ensures:
  • Transparent service pricing
  • No platform lock-in
  • Full multi-vendor flexibility
