Acceptable Use Policy (AUP).

Standards That Protect Stability, Security, and Operational Integrity.

Purpose of This Policy Core Principles of Responsible Use Prohibited Activities Security Expectations Network & Resource Integrity Content Responsibility & Ownership Email, Messaging & Communication Policies Fair Use & Resource Allocation Law Enforcement & Legal Obligations Violations & Enforcement Measures Customer Responsibilities Updates to This Policy A Platform Built on Responsibility Contact Information

Purpose of This Policy.

The Crafthost Acceptable Use Policy defines the rules that govern how customers may use our infrastructure, services, and network resources.

These standards ensure a safe, lawful, and stable environment for all organisations using the platform. Every customer is expected to adhere to these guidelines to maintain the integrity, security, and reliability of global operations.

This policy applies to all users, accounts, applications, workloads, and systems running on Crafthost infrastructure. By using Crafthost services, customers agree to follow the rules outlined across this document.

Core Principles of Responsible Use.

Crafthost infrastructure supports mission-critical workloads that require precision, reliability, and strong governance. To protect these environments, every use case must align with legal requirements, operational limits, and security best practices.

Activities that could harm systems, disrupt operations, or violate regulations are strictly prohibited.Customers are responsible for ensuring that their applications, content, and users operate within acceptable and lawful boundaries. These principles safeguard the continuity of services across all global regions.

Prohibited Activities.

Crafthost prohibits any activity that jeopardises security, stability, or lawful operation of the platform.The following actions are strictly forbidden across all environments:

/

Illegal or Unethical Conduct

Use of services to engage in unlawful, fraudulent, or deceptive activities, including unauthorised data access or misuse.

/

Security Violations

Attempts to bypass access controls, exploit vulnerabilities, or disrupt network performance through malicious behaviour.

/

Harmful or Malicious Software

Distribution or hosting of malware, ransomware, spyware, botnets, or any application intended to compromise systems.

/

Denial of Service or Abuse

Generation of traffic or behaviour designed to overload infrastructure, disrupt workloads, or impair service availability.

/

Intellectual Property Violations

Hosting or distributing materials in breach of copyrights, trademarks, patents, or other intellectual property protections.

/

Misrepre­sentation

Impersonation of individuals, organisations, or services for harmful or misleading purposes.

/

Open Relay or Unsolicited Messaging

Operation of email systems involved in spam distribution, open relays, or mass unsolicited communication.

/

Harmful Resource Consumption

Deploying workloads that intentionally degrade performance, destabilise infrastructure, or conflict with platform policies.

/

Child Exploitation Material

Crafthost enforces a zero-tolerance policy for any content that violates international child safety laws.

Each category is enforced to protect global network quality, customer environments, and operational safety.

Security Expectations.

Customers must maintain secure configurations across all workloads, applications, and authentication systems.Strong credential management, access control policies, and timely updates are required to protect operational integrity.

Misconfigured systems or insecure applications may threaten other environments and are subject to corrective action. Network-level protections should remain active to prevent abuse or unauthorised access. Continuous monitoring of customer-side environments is recommended as part of responsible operation.

Network & Resource Integrity.

Crafthost infrastructure is engineered for predictable performance and global availability.Customers must avoid behaviour that destabilises networks, causes excessive traffic spikes, or interferes with other users.

Traffic patterns must follow local regulations, industry norms, and operational boundaries defined by Crafthost.Use of automated tools or network-intensive scripts that degrade regional performance is prohibited. These standards ensure a stable environment for mission-critical applications.

Content Responsibility & Ownership.

Customers retain full responsibility for all content, applications, and data hosted within their environments. This includes compliance with laws related to privacy, distribution, and intellectual property. Crafthost does not monitor customer content except as required to enforce this policy or satisfy legal obligations.

Organisations must ensure their content does not contain harmful materials or violate legal frameworks. All hosted materials must align with acceptable and lawful digital practices.

Email, Messaging & Communication Policies.

Email and messaging services hosted on Crafthost must follow recognised best practices and avoid abusive behaviour.

Unsolicited bulk messages, deceptive communications, or misleading marketing materials are strictly prohibited. SMTP configurations must not operate as open relays or permit uncontrolled message distribution. Customers are responsible for email reputation management within their applications.These rules maintain network credibility and prevent spam-related service disruption.

Fair Use & Resource Allocation.

Crafthost infrastructure is designed to support consistent, high-performance workloads. To preserve this, customers must deploy workloads that follow fair resource distribution principles. Activities that degrade compute availability, consume excessive bandwidth, or affect regional performance may be restricted.

Resource-intensive processes should follow proper optimisation practices and align with allocated capacity. These boundaries protect the stability of all global customers.

Law Enforcement & Legal Obligations.

Crafthost complies with applicable laws and legitimate legal processes across all regions.Requests from authorities are evaluated for authenticity, jurisdiction, and adherence to legal frameworks. Customer information is disclosed only when required by law and handled with strict confidentiality. Users should maintain awareness of legal obligations within their industry and region. Crafthost collaborates responsibly with regulatory entities to preserve operational integrity.

Violations & Enforcement Measures.

If a customer violates the Acceptable Use Policy, Crafthost may take one or more actions depending on severity. These may include warnings, service limitations, workload isolation, suspension, or termination of services.In cases involving illegal activities, Crafthost may coordinate with law enforcement authorities.Resolution procedures prioritise the protection of customers, infrastructure, and network stability. Repeated or severe violations may result in permanent account restrictions.

Customer Responsibilities.

Customers must maintain compliance with this AUP and ensure that their users, employees, and applications do not violate its terms.

Regular audits, security checks, and policy reviews are recommended as part of responsible governance.Customers should promptly address vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, or abnormal behaviour within their systems.

Applications must follow industry best practices for authentication, encryption, and data protection.Adherence to these responsibilities strengthens both customer environments and overall platform reliability.

Updates to This Policy.

Crafthost may revise the Acceptable Use Policy periodically to align with regulatory changes, industry standards, or operational requirements.

Updated versions replace prior editions and will include a new effective date.Customers are encouraged to review this policy regularly to remain informed of any changes. Significant updates may be communicated through official channels when applicable. Continuous alignment ensures a stable, secure, and compliant operational environment.

A Platform Built on Responsibility.

Crafthost infrastructure is designed to support organisations that value reliability, governance, and operational confidence. This Acceptable Use Policy protects that environment by ensuring that all workloads align with ethical, legal, and secure digital practices. Our purpose is to provide a stable foundation for the institutions, enterprises, and innovators who trust Crafthost with their critical operations.

By following this policy, customers help maintain a secure, high-performance ecosystem that benefits every user across the platform.
