Crafthost Enterprise Hosting is designed for organizations where infrastructure is a business dependency, not an IT experiment.

What “Enterprise Hosting” Means at Crafthost.

Enterprise Hosting at Crafthost is not a product bundle. It is an operating model.

It means:

  • One accountable party for infrastructure outcomes
  • Defined responsibility boundaries
  • Governed change and escalation
  • Predictable operations under SLA

Crafthost assumes responsibility for operating, stabilizing, and coordinating infrastructure environments, while your organization retains strategic and architectural authority.

This operating model applies across multi-vendor and multi-platform environments—including private, dedicated, and hybrid estates—without creating platform lock-in or dependency on any single technology stack.
