Regulated Cloud.

Compliance-Driven Cloud Operations for Highly Regulated Enterprises.

Regulated Cloud at Crafthost is designed for organizations that operate under formal regulatory frameworks, where infrastructure must be operated in a way that is auditable, controlled, and demonstrably compliant.

This operating model is intended for environments where standards such as financial regulations, healthcare compliance, telecom obligations, or industry-specific controls shape how systems are designed, operated, and changed.

Crafthost delivers regulated cloud environments by aligning infrastructure operations, security execution, and change governance with external compliance requirements—without transferring operational risk back to internal teams.

This is cloud infrastructure operated for regulatory assurance, not experimentation.

What “Regulated Cloud” Means in Practice.

Regulated Cloud is not defined by where infrastructure runs. It is defined by how it is governed, operated, and evidenced.

At Crafthost, regulated cloud environments are characterized by:

  • Operational controls aligned to regulatory frameworks
  • Enforced change governance and approval workflows
  • Security execution mapped to compliance obligations
  • Continuous audit readiness across systems and operations
  • Clear accountability for regulatory execution outcomes

The objective is not certification alone, but operational alignment with regulatory expectations.

Why Conventional Cloud Operations Struggle Under Regulation.

Many cloud environments fail compliance not because of missing tools, but because of operational fragmentation.

Common failure patterns include:

  • Responsibility split across multiple vendors and teams
  • Change execution without traceable approval chains
  • Security controls defined but not operationally enforced
  • Audit preparation treated as a periodic event, not a steady state

Regulated Cloud at Crafthost addresses these gaps by embedding compliance into daily operations, not layering it on afterward.

Operating Controls Aligned to Regulatory Requirements.

Regulation demands evidence, not intent.
Crafthost enforces regulated operations through:

  • Structured change management with documented approvals
  • Incident handling with traceable timelines and ownership
  • Role-based access controls tied to compliance scopes
  • Documentation maintained as a living operational record
  • Security operations aligned to regulatory control sets

This model ensures that compliance can be demonstrated continuously, not reconstructed retrospectively.

Responsibility Model (Explicit and Auditable).

Regulated environments require clarity of execution responsibility.

Client responsibilities include:

  • Regulatory interpretation and policy ownership
  • Data classification and risk acceptance decisions
  • Business process compliance alignment

Crafthost responsibilities include:

  • Infrastructure availability and operational stability
  • Security control execution and monitoring
  • Change coordination and enforcement
  • Incident response and documentation support
  • Audit-ready operational records

Responsibility is not shared informally. It is assigned, documented, and enforced.

Where Regulated Cloud Is the Right Fit.

This operating model is intended for organizations such as:

Financial institutions and payment platforms.

Healthcare providers and health data processors.

Telecom operators and regulated service providers.

Energy and utilities organizations.

Enterprises subject to sector-specific regulatory oversight.

If regulatory requirements do not materially influence how infrastructure must be operated, this model may be excessive.

Regulated Cloud Compared to Standard Cloud Operations.

Standard cloud environments often assume:

  • Flexible change velocity
  • Tool-centric compliance
  • Internal responsibility for audit readiness

Regulated Cloud differs by introducing:

  • Governed change execution
  • Operational accountability for compliance controls
  • Continuous audit alignment as a baseline state

Operational Outcomes Observed in Regulated Environments.

These outcomes result from disciplined operations aligned to regulatory demands. Organizations operating under Crafthost’s regulated model have observed:

20–35% reduction in compliance-related operational escalations

Faster audit cycles due to pre-aligned documentation

Reduced change-related incidents through enforced governance

Improved consistency in security control execution

Engagement Model.

Regulated Cloud engagements follow a structured, compliance-first process:

  • Regulatory scope and control mapping
  • Definition of operational responsibilities
  • Architecture and governance alignment
  • Controlled onboarding and stabilization
  • Ongoing SLA-driven, compliance-aware operations

This model is designed for long-term regulatory alignment, not short-term infrastructure provisioning.

FAQ.

Is Regulated Cloud the same as Sovereign Cloud?

No. Regulated Cloud focuses on compliance execution and auditability. Sovereign Cloud additionally enforces jurisdictional and legal boundary constraints.

Does Regulated Cloud require specific certifications?

The operating model supports regulated environments regardless of certification paths. Certifications may apply depending on industry and jurisdiction, but operations are designed to align with regulatory expectations first.

Who handles audit preparation and evidence?

Crafthost maintains operational records and supports audit evidence related to infrastructure operations. Regulatory interpretation remains with the client.

Is this suitable for fast-moving development teams?

Only where development velocity can operate within governed change processes. This model prioritizes control over speed.

Can Regulated Cloud integrate with existing systems?

Yes, provided integrations respect defined security, access, and change governance boundaries.
