Service Level Agreement (SLA).

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

This Service Level Agreement (“SLA”) outlines the service commitments, availability targets, response times, maintenance standards, and operational expectations for customers using Crafthost’s infrastructure and managed services.

This SLA forms part of the Terms of Service and applies to all eligible Crafthost offerings unless otherwise stated in a separate written agreement.

1. Purpose of This SLA.

This SLA defines:

  • Service availability and uptime commitments
  • Support response times based on support tier
  • Performance expectations
  • Incident handling and escalation procedures
  • Maintenance windows
  • Customer responsibilities
  • Service exclusions

The goal is to ensure clarity, reliability, and predictable performance for organisations relying on Crafthost for mission-critical operations.

2. Covered Services.

This SLA applies to the following Crafthost services:

  • Managed Cloud VPS
  • Managed Dedicated Servers
  • High-Performance Dedicated Servers (AI, HPC, data-intensive workloads)
  • GPU AI Servers
  • Private Cloud Infrastructure
  • Hybrid Cloud Solutions
  • Migration Services
  • Enterprise Security Services
  • Network Services
  • Infrastructure Resources (compute, storage, network)

Additional services may be covered through customised agreements.

3. Infrastructure Availability.

Crafthost is committed to delivering high availability across its global platform.

Uptime Commitment

Unless stated otherwise in a service-specific agreement:

  • Standard Infrastructure Uptime: 99.9%
  • Managed Infrastructure Uptime: 99.95%
  • High-Performance & Enterprise Tiers: 99.99%

Availability is measured on a monthly basis and excludes approved maintenance windows and exceptions defined in Section 9.

Network Availability

Crafthost maintains a global network engineered with redundancy and multi-path routing.

  • Network Availability Target: 99.99%

Data Center Standards

Crafthost operates facilities aligned with Tier-IV and Tier-V engineering principles, ensuring:

  • Redundant power architecture
  • Multi-carrier connectivity
  • Physical security controls
  • Environmental stability
  • Continuous monitoring

Regional variations may apply based on data center design.

4. Performance Commitments.

Crafthost ensures:

  • Predictable compute performance
  • Low-latency network paths
  • High-speed storage access
  • Secure segmentation of customer workloads
  • Continuous monitoring of system health

Performance optimisation is included in Managed service tiers.

5. Support Tier Overview.

Crafthost ensures that data sharing is limited to situations where it is operationally or legally required.

Basic Support
  • Response within 72 hours
  • Ticket-based communication
  • Suitable for low-urgency environments
Standard Support
  • Response within 48 hours
  • Ticket and email assistance
  • Operational guidance included
Pro Support
  • Response within 24 hours
  • Priority handling
  • Enhanced assistance for business-critical operations
Advanced Support
  • Response within 1–2 hours
  • High-priority escalation
  • Ideal for enterprise environments requiring immediate attention

6. Incident Management.

Crafthost follows structured procedures during service-impacting events.
Incident Severity Levels:

/

Severity 1 — Critical

  • Complete service disruption affecting operations.
  • Action:
    Immediate investigation and continuous updates until resolved.
/

Severity 1 — High

  • Degraded performance affecting business functionality.
  • Action:
    Priority handling with targeted resolution.
/

Severity 1 — Medium

  • Non-critical issues with partial impact.
  • Action:
    Managed according to support tier timelines.
/

Severity 1 — Low

  • General inquiries, clarifications, or configuration questions.
  • Action:
    Resolved under standard procedures.

7. Scheduled Maintenance.

Crafthost conducts maintenance to ensure stability and security.

Planned Maintenance
  • Announced at least 48 hours in advance
  • Conducted during low-traffic windows
  • May cause minimal service interruption
Emergency Maintenance

Performed to prevent security risks or infrastructure instability.Notification is provided whenever possible.

8. Service Credits.

If availability falls below contractual commitments, eligible customers may request service credits.

Credit Eligibility

Credits apply only when:

  • Outage is confirmed by Crafthost
  • The customer opens a support ticket within 7 days of the event
  • The outage is not covered under exclusions

Credit Calculation

Credits are calculated as a percentage of the monthly fee for the affected service.
Example structure:

  • Uptime 99.0% – 99.89% → 5% credit
  • Uptime 98.0% – 98.99% → 10% credit
  • Uptime 95.0% – 97.99% → 20% credit
  • Uptime below 95.0% → 30% credit

Credits cannot exceed the monthly cost of the impacted service.

9. Exclusions.

This SLA does not cover downtime or events caused by:

  • Customer misconfiguration or misuse
  • Software installed by the customer
  • Third-party integrations
  • Malware originating from customer applications
  • Traffic floods or attacks exceeding mitigation thresholds
  • Force Majeure events
  • Suspension due to non-payment or policy violations
  • Maintenance windows announced by Crafthost
  • External network failures outside Crafthost’s control
  • Domain, DNS, or registry errors caused by third parties

10. Customer Responsibilities.

The customer agrees to:

  • Maintain accurate account information
  • Configure applications securely
  • Manage access and credentials responsibly
  • Update installed software regularly
  • Follow Crafthost’s Acceptable Use Policy
  • Ensure compliance with applicable laws
  • Provide cooperation during incident investigation

11. Data Backups.

Unless included within a purchased service plan:

Backup creation is the customer’s responsibility.

Crafthost is not liable for customer-side data loss.

Managed backup plans follow defined retention policies.

12. Changes to This SLA.

Crafthost may update this SLA to reflect:

Infrastructure improvements.

Regulatory changes.

Operational updates.

Revised versions take effect upon publication.

13. Governing Law.

This SLA is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which Crafthost is legally registered.
Any disputes will be handled through courts in that jurisdiction unless otherwise required by law.

14. Final Statement.

This SLA reflects Crafthost’s commitment to stability, precision, and responsibility across its global infrastructure. Organisations that rely on Crafthost receive predictable performance, disciplined engineering, and a service foundation built for long-term continuity.
