A Comprehensive System for Global Data Protection

Crafthost delivers infrastructure trusted by organisations that operate with strict regulatory obligations and heightened expectations for security and privacy.

This GDPR Data Protection & Processing Compliance Hub serves as a central reference for our unified data governance model, outlining how personal data is processed, protected, and managed across regions according to European standards.

Our infrastructure, operational policies, and governance controls are engineered to maintain lawful processing, defend data integrity, and ensure a transparent environment for businesses, institutions, and regulated industries.

Commitment to GDPR Compliance

Crafthost aligns its systems and operational practices with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), maintaining strict adherence to its core principles.

Every processing activity within our platform respects the rights of individuals, ensuring that personal data is handled with clarity, fairness, and lawful purpose.

GDPR principles embedded within our operations include:

  • Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency
  • Purpose limitation and minimisation
  • Data accuracy and quality control
  • Limited retention aligned with service needs
  • Security, confidentiality, and integrity of processing
  • Accountability through documentation and auditability

Crafthost ensures that customers receive an infrastructure that fully supports their regulatory obligations.

Lawful Processing & Transparent Use

Crafthost processes personal data solely when it is necessary for service delivery, legal compliance, or the execution of contractual obligations.

All processing is performed with clear, documented reasoning and communicated transparently to relevant stakeholders.

Lawful bases for processing may include:

  • Contractual necessity
  • Legitimate interests
  • Compliance with applicable laws
  • Explicit consent where applicable

We guarantee that all processing activities remain aligned with GDPR-defined requirements.

Data Minimisation & Purpose Limitation

Crafthost collects only the data required to operate, secure, and support our services.Personal data is processed strictly for its intended purpose and is never used for secondary, incompatible objectives.

Core purposes include:

  • Account creation and management
  • Service delivery for hosted workloads
  • Infrastructure performance and monitoring
  • Security, fraud prevention, and operational continuity
  • Legal and regulatory obligations

Our systems and processes enforce this principle across all service tiers.

Roles & Responsibilities Under GDPR

Customer as Data Controller

Customers determine how and why personal data is processed within their hosted environments.
They are responsible for lawful data collection, management of access rights, and defining processing instructions.

Crafthost as Data Processor

Crafthost processes personal data strictly under customer direction, without independent decision-making.
All processing follows documented instructions within the Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and GDPR requirements.

This separation ensures clarity, control, and regulatory consistency.

Technical & Organisational Security Measures

Crafthost applies advanced security controls to safeguard the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of personal data throughout its lifecycle.

Our protection measures include:

  • Encryption at rest and in transit
  • Multi-layer firewall and intrusion-prevention systems
  • Network segmentation and workload isolation
  • Tier-IV and Tier-V data centre principles
  • Identity and access control governance
  • Real-time event monitoring and logging
  • Regular vulnerability scanning and patch cycles
  • Secure software and infrastructure configurations

These measures prevent unauthorised access and support strong regulatory alignment.

Sub-Processors & Third-Party Compliance

Crafthost works with trusted sub-processors to maintain service delivery, infrastructure operations, and security capabilities.

Each sub-processor operates under strict contractual obligations, GDPR alignment requirements, and confidentiality standards.

Sub-processors are vetted for:

  • Security posture
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Confidentiality commitments
  • Technical competence
  • Alignment with Crafthost’s processing standards

A full list of sub-processors is available upon request.

International Data Transfers & Safeguards

When personal data is transferred across regions, Crafthost ensures that GDPR-equivalent protection remains in place.

International transfers rely on legally recognised mechanisms such as:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Adequacy decisions
  • Supplementary technical and organisational protections
  • Dedicated regional hosting options supporting sovereignty

These safeguards guarantee lawful, secure, and transparent data movement.

Data Subject Rights

Crafthost supports customer obligations to uphold the full range of data subject rights defined under GDPR, including:

  • Right of access
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to objection
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to restriction of processing

Crafthost assists controllers in addressing these rights efficiently and securely.

Breach Notification & Incident Response

If a personal data breach occurs, Crafthost will:

  • Notify affected customers without undue delay
  • Provide actionable information to support regulatory notifications
  • Assist in containment, mitigation, and recovery measures
  • Conduct a full internal investigation
  • Maintain transparency throughout all communication

Our processes align with GDPR-mandated urgency and reporting expectations.

Data Retention & Secure Deletion.

Crafthost retains personal data only for the duration required to deliver services or meet legal obligations.Upon termination or customer request, we ensure:

  • Secure deletion of customer personal data
  • Erasure of residual data through controlled lifecycle processes
  • Sanitisation of storage media in compliance with industry standards

All deletion operations are documented and follow robust internal governance practices.

Customer Responsibilities in Maintaining GDPR Compliance

While Crafthost maintains strong infrastructure-level safeguards, Customers remain responsible for:

  • Lawful data collection and consent handling
  • Application-level configuration and access management
  • Encryption key ownership where applicable
  • Ensuring correct, compliant processing instructions
  • Maintaining secure operational practices

This shared responsibility model provides both flexibility and control.

Audits, Documentation & Verification

Crafthost provides documentation to support internal audits, procurement checks, regulatory reviews, and security assessments.

Available documentation includes:

  • DPA terms and processing details
  • Security and operational control descriptions
  • Infrastructure compliance summaries
  • Data flow and processing diagrams (upon request)
  • Governance documentation under NDA

We support customers throughout their audit and compliance cycles.

Children’s Data & Restricted Processing Categories

Crafthost does not knowingly process personal data belonging to minors and does not provide services intended for individuals under legally defined age thresholds. Our systems are designed for enterprises and institutions that operate with formal regulatory responsibility.

Policy Updates & Version Control

Crafthost may update this Hub to reflect:

  • Regulatory changes
  • Operational adjustments
  • Infrastructure improvements
  • New compliance obligations
  • Security enhancements

All updates include revision dates for transparency and auditability.
