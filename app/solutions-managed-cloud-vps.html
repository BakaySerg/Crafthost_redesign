Managed Cloud VPS.

SLA-Driven Cloud Operations with Full Infrastructure Accountability.

Managed Cloud VPS at Crafthost is built for organizations that require predictable performance, governed operations, and contractual accountability—not unmanaged virtual machines or best-effort hosting.

This solution is designed for environments where infrastructure reliability directly affects revenue, compliance, and operational continuity. Crafthost assumes responsibility for the operation, stability, and lifecycle management of your cloud VPS environment, enabling internal teams to focus on applications, data, and strategic execution instead of infrastructure incidents.

We do not deliver virtual machines. We operate cloud environments.

Enterprise-only · Quote-based · SLA-driven · No instant checkout Request Managed Cloud VPS Assessment

What Managed Cloud VPS Means at Crafthost.

Managed Cloud VPS is an operational responsibility model, not a hosting plan.

Crafthost takes ownership of the cloud layer, including continuous monitoring, maintenance, security governance, controlled changes, and incident resolution under defined SLAs.

This solution is intended for organizations that:

  • Require contractual uptime commitments
  • Operate under regulatory or compliance constraints
  • Need consistent performance under sustained workloads
  • Cannot accept fragmented responsibility between vendors
  • Expect senior engineering involvement during incidents

Operational responsibility is externalized while architectural authority and strategic control remain internal.

Built for Stability, Not Self-Service Scale.

Unlike commodity VPS platforms optimized for rapid provisioning, Managed Cloud VPS prioritizes:

  • Operational stability
  • Controlled capacity growth
  • Predictable latency and I/O behavior
  • Governance and disciplined change management
  • Documented operational standards

This operating model eliminates common failure patterns such as configuration drift, noisy-neighbor risk, and unclear escalation paths.

Scope of Managed Responsibility.

Crafthost assumes responsibility across the full cloud VPS lifecycle.
This is not a self-service VPS offering. Crafthost operates the cloud layer end-to-end, assuming responsibility for stability, security, and lifecycle outcomes—so internal teams are not forced into day-to-day infrastructure operations.

Infrastructure Operations

  • Cloud VPS provisioning and lifecycle management
  • Multi-zone and multi-region architecture support
  • Resource tuning and capacity optimization
  • OS-level hardening and baseline configuration

Availability & Reliability

  • SLA-backed availability targets
  • Continuous health monitoring
  • Proactive incident prevention
  • Root-cause analysis and permanent remediation

Security & Governance

  • Secure baseline enforcement
  • Access control governance
  • Patch coordination and vulnerability management
  • Auditable change management

Operational Visibility

  • Clear incident ownership
  • Risk-based operational reporting
  • Defined escalation paths
  • Direct access to senior engineers when required

Who This Solution Is Not For.

Managed Cloud VPS is not designed for:

  • Hobby projects or experimental workloads
  • Cost-driven hosting decisions
  • Environments requiring infrastructure access only
  • Teams seeking instant checkout or self-service provisioning

Organizations that need infrastructure without operational ownership should refer to Standard Cloud VPS pricing.
This separation exists to protect reliability, accountability, and service quality.

Organizations in early experimentation stages may prefer infrastructure-only cloud VPS. Standard Cloud VPS options are available under Pricing.

When Managed Cloud VPS Is the Right Choice.

Managed Cloud VPS is the right fit when:

  • Downtime carries financial or regulatory consequences
  • Internal teams should not manage infrastructure day-to-day
  • Cloud environments require governance and accountability
  • Executive stakeholders expect predictable operations

Engagement Model.

Crafthost does not offer instant checkout or generic packages for managed cloud operations. Every engagement begins with an Enterprise Infrastructure Assessment, conducted by senior engineers to evaluate:

  • Architecture and dependencies
  • Risk exposure and failure points
  • Operational maturity
  • SLA and governance requirements

Based on this assessment, responsibility boundaries and service scope are defined.

Managed Cloud VPS FAQ.

How is Managed Cloud VPS different from standard VPS hosting?

Managed Cloud VPS includes full operational ownership by Crafthost. Standard VPS provides infrastructure access only.

Is this suitable for regulated industries?

Yes. The service supports compliance-aligned operations, governance controls, and audit-ready practices.

Can you manage existing cloud VPS environments?

Yes. Existing environments can be onboarded following assessment and operational alignment.

Do customers retain control?

Yes. Customers retain ownership and strategic control. Crafthost assumes operational responsibility.

Is pricing fixed?

No. Pricing is assessment-based and reflects architecture, risk, SLA depth, and operational scope.
