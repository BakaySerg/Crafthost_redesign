Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy Scope of This Privacy Policy Data We Collect How Your Data Is Used Legal Basis for Processing Data Sharing and Disclosure Data Security International Data Transfers Data Retention Your Rights Cookies & Tracking Technologies Third-Party Links Children’s Privacy Changes to This Privacy Policy Final Statement

Effective Date: [16/12/2025]

Crafthost is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of every individual and organisation that interacts with our services. This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, processed, stored, and protected across all platforms, systems, and services offered by Crafthost.

By accessing or using Crafthost’s website, infrastructure, or related services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors accessing any Crafthost website
  • Customers using managed hosting, cloud, dedicated, GPU, hybrid, or private cloud services
  • Individuals contacting support or submitting inquiries
  • Users participating in webinars, downloads, knowledge base access, or documentation portals
  • Applicants applying for roles through the careers section

This policy does not apply to third-party services integrated or chosen independently by customers.

2. Data We Collect

Crafthost collects information necessary to provide secure, reliable, and compliant infrastructure services.

Data Provided Directly by You
  • Account registration information
  • Contact details submitted through forms
  • Billing or invoicing information
  • Support tickets and correspondence
  • Documentation, attachments, or files voluntarily submitted
  • Job applications or professional materials
Data Collected Automatically
  • IP address
  • Browser type, device type, and system configuration
  • Access timestamps and usage logs
  • Landing pages, referral sources, and navigation metrics
  • Security logs, authentication attempts, and system events
Service and Infrastructure Data

When using hosted services, Crafthost may collect operational metadata required to maintain performance, security, uptime, and compliance.
Examples include:

  • Network activity logs (latency, routing, performance metrics)
  • Resource utilization metrics
  • Infrastructure alerts and diagnostics
  • Availability and service-health reports

Crafthost does not access customer content stored on servers unless required for security, lawful compliance, or explicit technical assistance.

3. How Your Data Is Used

Crafthost processes collected data for the following purposes:

  • Delivering managed hosting and cloud infrastructure services
  • Ensuring uptime, performance, and operational stability
  • Strengthening platform security and preventing malicious activity
  • Responding to support requests and resolving issues
  • Handling billing, invoicing, and account management
  • Improving service quality and user experience
  • Conducting analytics to optimise performance
  • Complying with legal, regulatory, and audit obligations
  • Reviewing applications submitted through the careers section

No data is sold, leased, or traded.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Crafthost processes personal data under legitimate, lawful foundations:

Contractual necessity for delivering services.

Legitimate interests in securing and operating infrastructure.

Legal obligations related to compliance and regulatory frameworks.

Explicit consent where required.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Crafthost limits data sharing to situations where it is operationally or legally required.

Service Providers

Data may be shared with carefully selected entities that assist with:

  • Billing operations
  • Customer support tools
  • Security and DDoS mitigation
  • System monitoring
  • Backup and disaster recovery
  • Compliance assessments
Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed when required to:

  • Comply with applicable law
  • Respond to government or regulatory requests
  • Comply with court orders or lawful investigations
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or security risks
Business Continuity

In the event of structural or organisational changes, data may be transferred only if protected under equal or stronger privacy terms.

6. Data Security

Crafthost implements multi-layer security across every part of the infrastructure:

  • Encryption for data in transit and at rest
  • Continuous security monitoring
  • Tier-IV and Tier-V data center principles
  • Redundant power and network architecture
  • Strict access control and authentication
  • Firewall and intrusion-prevention systems
  • Infrastructure-level DDoS protection
  • Regular security audits and compliance checks

Security is a foundational requirement across all Crafthost environments.

7. International Data Transfers

Crafthost operates distributed infrastructure across Europe, North America, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia-Pacific.

Data may be processed within any region where Crafthost or its data center partners operate, always under:

Appropriate legal safeguards.

Contractual protections.

Industry-standard security controls.

8. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for:

Service delivery

Operational continuity

Regulatory requirements

Legal obligations

Security and fraud prevention

When no longer necessary, data is securely removed or anonymised.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may request:

  • Access to personal data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of data under certain conditions
  • Restriction of processing
  • Export of data in portable format
  • Withdrawal of consent when applicable

Requests may be submitted via the contact methods provided at the end of this policy.

10. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Crafthost uses cookies and similar tools to:

  • Enhance user experience
  • Maintain login sessions
  • Improve website performance
  • Analyse page usage for optimisation
  • Secure interactions and detect anomalies

Requests may be submitted via the contact methods provided at the end of this policy.

The Crafthost website may contain external links. Crafthost is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.
Users are encouraged to review individual policies when navigating outside the Crafthost domain.

12. Children’s Privacy

Crafthost does not target services toward minors and does not knowingly collect data from individuals under applicable age thresholds.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Crafthost may update this Privacy Policy to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes.
Revised versions will include an updated effective date.

14. Final Statement

Crafthost is committed to maintaining the highest standards of data protection, transparency, and trust.
Our privacy practices are designed to support the organisations that depend on secure and reliable infrastructure across global regions.

Contact Information.

For questions, requests, or concerns related to privacy, users may contact Crafthost.
Crafthost handles all privacy inquiries with professionalism, confidentiality, and structural compliance.
