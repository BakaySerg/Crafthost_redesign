This Cookie Policy explains how Crafthost (“we”, “our”, “the Company”) uses cookies and similar technologies to recognise visitors, improve performance, secure interactions, and enhance user experience across our websites and platforms.
By continuing to browse or use Crafthost’s website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. Purpose of Cookies

Cookies help ensure that Crafthost’s website operates efficiently, remains secure, and delivers relevant content. These technologies assist with:

  • Maintaining website functionality
  • Securing login sessions
  • Understanding user behaviour for optimisation
  • Improving page performance and load times
  • Enabling personalised elements
  • Detecting unusual or malicious activities

Cookies are essential for delivering a consistent and reliable browsing experience.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website.
They allow websites to recognise returning visitors, remember preferences, and track activity necessary for performance, analytics, and security enhancement.
Cookies used by Crafthost do not give access to personal files, nor do they allow control of your device.

3. Types of Cookies Used by Crafthost.

Crafthost uses several categories of cookies to deliver a safe and seamless online experience.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the core operation of the website and cannot be disabled.
They support:

  • Account login and session management
  • Form submissions
  • Navigation and functionality
  • Fraud prevention and session integrity
  • Secure platform access

Disabling these cookies may prevent the website from functioning correctly.

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help Crafthost understand how visitors interact with the website.
They collect information about:

  • Page views and navigation patterns
  • Traffic sources and visitor behaviour
  • Load times and technical performance
  • System errors or browser issues

This data allows Crafthost to optimise the user experience and enhance platform stability.

Functionality Cookies

These cookies remember user choices and improve usability by:

  • Saving language preferences
  • Remembering interface settings
  • Retaining form data for faster interactions
  • Supporting improved accessibility

Functionality cookies ensure a smoother and more personalised experience.

Security Cookies

Used to strengthen protection across all sessions.
They support:

  • Detection of unauthorised access attempts
  • Enhanced authentication processes
  • Prevention of malicious activity
  • Monitoring and safeguarding critical entry points

Security cookies are crucial for protecting both users and infrastructure.

Advertising & Marketing Cookies

If marketing tools are used, these cookies may:

  • Tailor promotional content
  • Provide relevant product recommendations
  • Measure campaign effectiveness
  • Support targeted advertising

Crafthost does not sell or trade data collected via cookies.

4. Third-Party Cookies.

Some pages may include tools or integrations from trusted third parties, such as:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Security services
  • Performance monitoring systems
  • Embedded documentation or video players
  • These providers may set their own cookies to deliver their functions

Crafthost carefully assesses all third-party partners and ensures they adhere to strong privacy and security commitments.

Cookies may be stored for:

Session Duration

Defined Periods

6. Managing or Disabling Cookies.

You may control cookies through browser settings. Options usually include:

  • Accepting or blocking all cookies

  • Deleting stored cookies
  • Receiving notifications when cookies are used

However, disabling certain cookies may impact website functionality, security, or performance.
For detailed steps, please refer to your browser’s help or settings documentation.
